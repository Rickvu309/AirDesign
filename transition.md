# Transition

How a slide arrives when presenting.

## Language

**transition**:
How a slide arrives when presenting: its style, how long that style takes, and whether the next slide waits for a click or follows after a delay.
_Avoid_: animation, effect

**Animate**:
The tab of the design panel that sets the transition of a slide, or of every slide selected in grid view in one undo step. The other tab, Design, sets the slide background and the template style.
_Avoid_: motion, effects
