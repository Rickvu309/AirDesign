# A group's undo pressed before the tab has seen the other tab's nudge catches up, keeps the nudged box, and shows no notice; a two-tab browser spec waits until the tab that presses a key paints the other tab's edit

`doom-undo-stray-box.test.ts` has two tabs group a layer, one nudge its box, and the other undo the group. When that undo is one revision behind the nudge, `drain` in `packages/live/src/doom-deck.ts` catches it up and resends the writes that still stand. After sync the card is back in the frame, the box stays at the nudge, the group is gone, and no notice shows. Redo puts the card back in the group and keeps that box. The same undo lands with no notice once the tab has already caught up. A browser spec that presses a key in a tab after the other tab's edit waits until both tabs paint the same layers: `tests/browser/editor-undo-deleted-layer.spec.ts` compares the painted box, or the order of `[data-node]`, in both tabs. A read of the saved deck on the server shows only that the edit landed, not that the second tab has it. Without the waits, three undo specs failed 1 to 3 runs in 8 under 32 busy CPU loops (`editor-undo-deleted-layer.spec.ts` at 401, 411 and 454).
