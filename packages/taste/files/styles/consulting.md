# Consulting

For business, finance and strategy material read by executives: board updates, quarterly reviews, market sizing, cost and pricing cases.

## Identity

The reader is a senior person who has to decide something. The deck reads like the work of a strategy firm: each slide argues one claim, the evidence sits right under it, and nothing is there for decoration. Calm, exact, a little austere. The serif title carries authority; the sans body keeps numbers easy to read.

## Layout

- Every content slide uses the same frame: title at the top left across the full width, evidence in the body, source line at the bottom left.
- Align everything left. Centre only a single headline number.
- The body is a chart, a table, or a chart with two or three takeaways in a column on its right, about 60 to 40. The takeaways are plain text on white, with no panel behind them.
- Compare options in two or three plain columns with a `heading` on each, not in cards.
- Tables: header row in `label`, hairline rules between rows, numbers right-aligned, no row fills, no outer border.

## Token map

- `background`: every slide. Stay on white.
- `text`: titles, body and table figures.
- `text2`: secondary words: axis labels, column headers, notes, the source line.
- `accent1`: the one thing the title talks about: one bar, one row, one figure. One per slide.
- `accent2`: totals, the base series, and the fill of a single callout with `background` text.
- `text+75`: every other bar or series, so the highlighted one stands out.
- `text+85`: hairlines: table rules, axis lines, dividers.
- `background2`: the fill of a single callout, as tall as its words.
- `accent3`, `accent4`: a second and third series, only when the chart compares series.
- `positive`, `negative`: gains and losses in a bridge or a variance column.
- Type: `title` for titles, `heading` for a column head, `body` for points, `body2` for table cells, `caption` for the source and notes, `label` for table headers and axis titles, `bigNumber` for a headline figure.

## Decoration

- Allowed: hairline rules, corners square or radius 4 at most, a number in a small `accent2` square for a numbered recommendation.
- Not allowed: shadows or any `effect`, gradients, icons, mood pictures, tinted slide backgrounds, rounded cards, a box taller than its words, more than one highlight colour on a slide.

## Hierarchy

1. The title, which states the claim.
2. The one element in the accent, which proves it.
3. The rest of the evidence, in neutral.
4. Source and notes, smallest and quietest.

Someone who reads only the title and the coloured element gets the point.

## Typography

- Titles in `title`: Source Serif 4 at 60, sentence case, two lines at most.
- Body in Source Sans 3: `body` for two to four points, `body2` for tables and dense lists. Nothing under `caption`.

## Parameters

- `accent`: the colour for the point that matters. Default `accent1`, blue. Use `accent6`, red, when the point is a problem or a risk.
- `density`: `standard` (default): `body` text and up to four points. `dense`: `body2`, tables up to ten rows, for detail and appendix slides.
