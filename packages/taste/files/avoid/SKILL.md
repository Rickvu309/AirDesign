# What to avoid on slides

These are the marks of a slide an AI made without looking. Each one costs the reader's trust.

## Layout

- Don't park a row of cards at the top and leave the lower half empty. Fill the body area.
- Don't put every element in its own card. Plain text on the slide is fine; a card groups things that belong together.
- Don't give every slide the same layout. Let the content choose: a chart, a table, two columns, one statement.
- Don't put two charts on one slide. Two charts make two points.

## Titles and words

- Don't write topic titles such as "Overview" or "Q3 results". State what happened.
- Don't draw an accent line or bar under a title.
- Don't add a subtitle that repeats the title.
- Don't write walls of text: five bullets at most, a line or two each.
- Don't end with "Thank you", "Questions?" or "Key takeaways". End with the ask.

## Colour and decoration

- Don't keep the starter blue out of habit. Pick the theme that suits the subject.
- Don't use purple or blue-to-purple gradients, glows, neon or glass.
- Don't colour every bar differently. One accent marks the item the title talks about; the rest stay neutral.
- Don't add icons as decoration. Use one only when it says something a word cannot.
- Don't draw logos, illustrations or mascots out of shapes. No emoji.

## Numbers

- Don't show a number without its unit and its source.
- Don't shrink text or line spacing to make content fit. Split the slide.
