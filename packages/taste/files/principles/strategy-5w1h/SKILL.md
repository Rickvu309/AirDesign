# Strategy in six questions

A plan is something a new leader could pick up and run. A vision, a list of values or a bare list of goals is not yet a plan. Answer six questions, each on a slide or as a row of one table.

| Question | A complete answer                                                                                                         |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| How      | The levers, each an action with an object: "double the field sales team", never a theme like "grow sales".                |
| Who      | An owner per lever, the people or money asked for, who signs off, and each dependency on another team as an explicit ask. |
| What     | The metric per goal and the value that counts as success, with a leading indicator you can see early.                     |
| Why      | The goal behind the goal, and what you chose not to do, each with its reason.                                             |
| When     | The deadline, dated checkpoints, and the risks that could move them.                                                      |
| Which    | The segment, market or customer the plan focuses on, and the evidence for that choice.                                    |

## Checks

- [ ] Every lever has an owner, a metric with a target, and a date.
- [ ] Every dependency on another team appears as an ask.
- [ ] The not-doing list exists, with a reason per item.
- [ ] A non-obvious recommendation shows how you reached it: the judgment calls, the assumptions and the evidence.
- [ ] Principles and trade-offs come before tactics; channels, cadences and escalation owners wait for the appendix or a later slide.
- [ ] Asking "why" a few times from any lever reaches the business goal.
