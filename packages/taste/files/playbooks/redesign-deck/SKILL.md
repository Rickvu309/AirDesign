# Redesign deck

The person's content is the material: their numbers, names and claims stay exactly as given. What changes is the order, the titles and the form. Read the whole deck with `get()` before you touch a slide.

Work freely. At each pause point, stop and confirm every item. Fix what fails before you go on.

## Read on these triggers

- Rewriting titles: `principles/headlines`.
- A slide shows a number or a chart: `principles/number-truth`.
- Cutting, merging or reordering slides and lists: `principles/one-two-three`.

## Pieces that fit this job

Options, chosen by the form each old slide should take. Copy them from `kit`; each recipe is a script you run.

- Titles in one place on every slide: `kit/title-block`.
- A pie, a donut or nested circles becomes sorted bars: `charts/bar`, or one share bar: `kit/share-box`.
- A gridded or banded table becomes a minimal one: `charts/table`.
- Periods: `charts/column` or `charts/line`.
- A run of highlight slides over one exhibit becomes one slide: `kit/numbered-callout`.
- Boxes and arrows drawn by hand become a recipe: `diagrams/process-flow` or `diagrams/architecture`.
- Sources: `kit/source-line`.

## Pause 1: Setup

- [ ] The ask and the mode are recovered from the old deck or the brief, and the ask is one sentence.
- [ ] The deck keeps its style unless that style is broken; a broken one is replaced by the style its job calls for.
- [ ] Every number in the old deck is listed, so none changes or goes missing by accident.

## Pause 2: Content

- [ ] Each slide passes the remove test: without it, the story breaks. Slides that fail are merged or cut.
- [ ] Material that is merely interesting moves to an appendix.
- [ ] Lists are reordered so the most important item comes first.
- [ ] A data dump becomes groups with what to do about each.

## Pause 3: Headlines

- [ ] Every label title is now a claim with a verb.
- [ ] The answer moved to the front; the titles alone tell the argument.
- [ ] Jargon titles are rewritten in plain words.

## Pause 4: Visuals

- [ ] None of the old forms remain: pies, 3D, nested circles, gridded tables, animations, highlight sequences.
- [ ] Decorative icons, clip art and title underlines are gone.
- [ ] Green, amber and red match what each result means; one accent per slide.
- [ ] Every number matches the old deck, or the change is stated.
