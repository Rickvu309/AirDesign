# An agent's shape with no box takes the next free slot of a three by two grid in the band between the title or subtitle and the slide's footer, and is refused with a request for a box only when no slot or free room is left

`packages/doom/src/agent-shape-slots.test.ts` fails unless one, three and six shapes with no box, added one by one or in one batch, on a cover and on a titled slide, each land below the title or subtitle and above the footer, inside the title's measure, apart from each other, in reading order and with no layout finding, and unless a seventh is refused `limit` on `box` with a reason that asks for a box (verdict 1580-46f8392, finding 1). Inside a frame, a shape with no box takes the frame's free room, as before the grid, so a 50 by 20 frame still takes one (verdict 1580-de43799, finding 4). A title narrowed below three slots' width leaves the shape the slide's free room (verdict 1580-722151a, finding 2).
