# A chart, timeline, flow or matrix fills the band down to the bottom margin, and text tiles stay at content height, never taller than their box

`packages/doom/src/agent-band.test.ts` builds a titled slide for a timeline that runs down, a flow that runs down, a matrix and a lone chart component and fails unless each ends on the bottom margin. It builds a timeline that runs across, a flow that runs across, KPI tiles and cards and fails unless those stay shorter than the margin. It gives KPI tiles, cards and compare a box twice as tall as their words and fails unless the row keeps the box's top and width, stays under half its height and hugs its tallest card with every card as tall as the row. It gives KPI tiles a box shorter than their measured words and fails unless the row steps its words down into the box or refuses, and it sweeps long KPI, card and compare words through every built-in theme and fails if a measured row paints past its box. `apps/api/src/mcp/card-row-ceiling.test.ts` adds the same rows through `edit_deck` with served font widths and fails if one paints past its box. `packages/doom/src/agent-grid.test.ts` builds a word grid and fails unless its rows hug and its gap equals the slide spacing step.
