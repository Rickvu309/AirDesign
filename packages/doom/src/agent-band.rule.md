# A chart, timeline, flow or matrix fills the band down to the bottom margin, and text tiles stay at content height, even in a taller box

`packages/doom/src/agent-band.test.ts` builds a titled slide for a timeline that runs down, a flow that runs down, a matrix and a lone chart component and fails unless each ends on the bottom margin. It builds a timeline that runs across, a flow that runs across, KPI tiles and cards and fails unless those stay shorter than the margin. It gives KPI tiles, cards and compare a box twice as tall as their words and fails unless the row keeps the box's top and width, stays under half its height with every card as tall as the row, and raises no `empty-card` finding. `packages/doom/src/slide-findings.test.ts` stands a KPI row far taller than its words and fails unless one `empty-card` finding names the row, and stays quiet for a row its fullest card fills and for cards with no fill. `packages/doom/src/agent-patterns.test.ts` builds a word grid and fails unless its rows hug and its gap equals the slide spacing step.
