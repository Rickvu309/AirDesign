# An elbow line between two shapes crosses neither, leaving and entering each on its own side, unless a site is wedged in a narrow gap

`packages/doom/src/line-route.ts` finds the route by searching only moves that stay out of both shapes' bounds; when the bounds overlap, it searches again around the shapes' true outlines (turned shapes and preset outlines such as a triangle's), leaving each site where it stands, so no case can be forgotten. Two shapes whose facing sides touch and share part of that edge take one plain line along it; sides that sit level but share no edge take the stubbed route. When the two sites meet, the line keeps both ends, a line of no length, and `resolve-slide.ts` draws it with no arrowhead, since it has no direction to point. `packages/doom/src/line-route-sweep.test.ts` routes every side pair between five common presets at gaps from 0 to 60 px, behind, beside and overlapping, and between shapes turned 30, 45 and 90 degrees, and fails on any leg inside either true outline, or a route that leaves or arrives off its side. A pair is exempt only when leaving a site enters the other shape at once. Two cases still cross, each only where a site sits in a narrow diagonal wedge between its own outline and the other shape, and the sweep pins each count and distance. A level diamond onto a triangle crosses with the outlines 11 px apart or less, or overlapping: 16 of 387 routes in its sample, and 7,804 of 3.4M at 1 px steps over four size pairs. Shapes turned off the quarter cross in at most 17 of about 4,800 routes of the main grid, all with the outlines 11 px apart or less. A denser survey (2 px steps, six pairs, 1.2M routes) finds 0.6 percent crossing, none with the outlines more than 20 px apart; for two shapes turned 30 degrees, at 1 px steps, the most is 15 px apart (14,897 of 590,582 routes), and a sample of 377 routes pins 15. In some of these no route of level and upright legs exists at all, since every leg from the site meets one shape or the other. In the rest a stepped route exists, but it turns where the router has no lane: the router turns only on lines through the sites, stubs of 24, 12 or 6 px, and the outlines' corners. There the line goes straight through. The caps only go down.
