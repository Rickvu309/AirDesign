# An elbow line between two shapes crosses neither, leaving and entering each on its own side, unless a site is wedged in a narrow gap

`packages/doom/src/line-route.ts` finds the route by searching only moves that stay out of both shapes' bounds; when the bounds overlap, it searches again around the shapes' true outlines (turned shapes and preset outlines such as a triangle's), leaving each site where it stands, so no case can be forgotten. Two shapes whose facing sides touch and share part of that edge take one plain line along it; sides that sit level but share no edge take the stubbed route. When the two sites meet, the line keeps both ends, a line of no length, and `resolve-slide.ts` draws it with no arrowhead, since it has no direction to point. `packages/doom/src/line-route-sweep.test.ts` routes every side pair between five common presets at gaps from 0 to 60 px, behind, beside and overlapping, and between shapes turned 30, 45 and 90 degrees, and fails on any leg inside either true outline, or a route that leaves or arrives off its side. A pair is exempt only when leaving a site enters the other shape at once. Two groups of cases still cross, each only where a site sits in a narrow diagonal wedge between its own outline and the other shape, and the sweep pins the route count, the failure cap and the outline distance cap of each in `line-route-sweep.test.ts`; the numbers live there and nowhere else, and the caps only go down. The groups are shapes turned off the quarter (a main grid, plus a sample of two rectangles turned 30 degrees) and a level diamond onto a triangle. The sweep does not cover every pair: elbows can still cross their own shapes when the outlines are close and no level route exists, and no bound is promised for them. The worst pairs known are a rectangle turned 45 degrees onto another rectangle turned 45 degrees, a triangle onto a diamond, and an ellipse onto a triangle. In some of these no route of level and upright legs exists at all, since every leg from the site meets one shape or the other. In the rest a stepped route exists, but it turns where the router has no lane: the router turns only on lines through the sites, stubs of 24, 12 or 6 px, and the outlines' corners. There the line goes straight through.
