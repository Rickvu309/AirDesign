# An undo puts a layer back only where it shows and outside itself, so a frame that is gone or now sits inside the layer never holds the steps below it

`onStandingLayers` in `packages/doom/src/standing-layers.ts` is the one judge of what an undo may put back, for a person's step (`invert` in `packages/doom/src/invert.ts`) and for an agent's run (`invertRun` in `packages/doom/src/invert-run.ts`). It leaves out every write to a layer whose parent is `null` unless the same undo puts that layer back, and every move whose frame, or a frame above it, is deleted, or that would put the layer inside itself. It judges a move only once the moves of the frames above its target are judged, so leaving one move out never drops another that still lands. The deck refuses those writes as `missing` or `cycle`, or lands the layer inside a deleted frame where nobody sees it, and `turn` in `packages/live/src/doom-deck.ts` keeps a refused step on top, so one such write made every later Ctrl+Z refused for good (verdict 1494-0ca0e9e, finding 5; verdict 1510-559d9e2, finding 3). A step with nothing left is taken off and answers `nothing`, as Figma does nothing for an undo of an edit to a layer someone else deleted, and the next press reaches the step below. `NeverStuck` in `specs/undo-steps/UndoSteps.tla` fails in 50 states without the deleted-layer rule, and `specs/undo-moves/UndoMoves.tla` fails `NeverLosesAStep` in 48 states and `NeverStuck` in 54 with the old rule, which checked only the frame itself and kept a `cycle` step on top. `tests/undo-steps-replay.test.ts` fails 48 edges and `tests/undo-moves-replay.test.ts` 41 of 401 on the old `invert`, and `packages/doom/src/invert.test.ts`, `packages/doom/src/invert-run.test.ts`, `packages/live/src/doom-undo-deleted-layer.test.ts`, `packages/live/src/doom-undo-steps.test.ts` and `tests/browser/doom-undo-deleted-layer.spec.ts` fail when a deleted or nested frame holds an undo or a redo.
