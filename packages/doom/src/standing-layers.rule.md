# An undo never hides a layer that shows, never drops a visible edit to a layer it leaves in place, never loses a deleted layer whose frames come back, and never deletes a frame that still holds a layer

`onStandingLayers` in `packages/doom/src/standing-layers.ts` is the one judge of what an undo may put back, for a person's step (`invert` in `packages/doom/src/invert.ts`) and for an agent's run (`invertRun` in `packages/doom/src/invert-run.ts`). It leaves out every write to a layer whose parent is `null` unless the same undo puts that layer back, a move into a deleted frame or into the layer itself, and, while the layer shows, a move into a frame under a deleted one, so Ctrl+Z never makes a layer vanish into a frame someone else deleted. A layer that does not show, because it is deleted or sits under a deleted frame, goes back into its old frame even under a deleted one, and comes back when that frame does: delete a card in Outer > Inner, another tab deletes Outer, and Ctrl+Z puts the card back into Inner, so their Ctrl+Z brings Outer, Inner and the card back (verdict 1514-a78b019, finding 2). Strict Figma would lose the card there, since their delete took Inner with Outer and an undo into a deleted frame does nothing; AirDesign keeps it, as `main` did. A deleted layer goes back even into the deleted frame itself: `write` in `packages/doom/src/apply-edit.ts` lands a move into a deleted frame only for a write that takes back a delete (`takesBackDelete`), one that names the edit it replaces as its `base`, as every undo, redo and run undo does, of a layer deleted before the edit. The layer stays hidden there and comes back with the frame, so a card deleted from Inner while another tab deletes Inner comes back when they bring Inner back (verdict 1514-d30cef9, finding 6). Every other move there is refused `missing`: an ordinary move, such as a reorder sent offline or from a tab that has not caught up after another tab deleted the layer and then its frame, the move of a layer that shows or is only hidden, and a new layer, even one the same edit deleted first. When that reorder landed, their Ctrl+Z on the frame brought the layer back too, and their next Ctrl+Z did nothing (verdict 1533-b022c58, findings 1 and 2). `NeverLosesALayer` in `UndoDeletedFrames.tla` fails in 56 states when the deck refuses that restore, as `main` did, and `NeverLosesAStep` in 30 when any move of K into a deleted frame lands; its replay fails 10 of 473 edges and `tests/undo-frames-replay.test.ts` 7 of 497 on the old refusal. `specs/undo-pending-moves/UndoPendingMoves.tla` fails `OneStepAtATime` in 96 states when any write may move a deleted layer into a deleted frame (b022c58), and `NeverLosesALayer` in 58 when no write may (`main`); `tests/undo-pending-moves-replay.test.ts` fails 8 of 498 edges on the first and 51 on the second. For a layer whose move it leaves out, it leaves out only where the layer sits in its frame: `x`, `y`, `rotation` and the flips of `box`, and the point ends of a line's `geometry`, which are measured in the frame. Every other write still lands, the layer's size among them, so an agent run that moved a card out of a frame since deleted and painted it red, retyped it or resized it gets its colour, words and size back (verdict 1514-a78b019, findings 1 and 3). It judges a move only once the moves of the frames above its target are judged, so leaving one move out never drops another that still lands. It leaves out the delete of a frame that would still hold a layer: one whose move it left out, or one someone moved in after the step (`heldFrames`). The deck refuses the writes it leaves out as `missing` or `cycle`, or lands a showing layer where nobody sees it, and `turn` in `packages/live/src/doom-deck.ts` keeps a refused step on top, so one such write made every later Ctrl+Z refused for good (verdict 1494-0ca0e9e, finding 5; verdict 1510-559d9e2, finding 3). A step with nothing left is taken off and answers `nothing`, as Figma does nothing for an undo of an edit to a layer someone else deleted, and the next press reaches the step below. An agent run's undo names the layers whose move it left out (`left`), and answers `left` when nothing else lands: the Agents panel says each stays where it is because the frame it came from was deleted or moved, never that nothing was left to undo, and Aira's chat says so beside Undone. `specs/undo-deleted-frames/UndoDeletedFrames.tla` fails `NeverLosesALayer` in 54 states with the rule that checked every frame above for a layer that does not show, and `NeverDropsAVisibleEdit` in 44 states when a layer left in place lost its other writes; its replay fails 63 of 469 edges on a78b019's judge, 50 with only the other writes dropped and 13 with only the frames above checked. `NeverStuck` in `specs/undo-steps/UndoSteps.tla` fails in 50 states without the deleted-layer rule. `specs/undo-moves/UndoMoves.tla` fails `NeverLosesAStep` in 48 states with the rule that checked only the frame itself, and `NeverStuck` in 54 when a step refused `cycle` stays on top. `specs/undo-frames/UndoFrames.tla` fails `NeverLosesALayer` in 62 states when every delete lands and in 72 when only a left-out move keeps a frame. `tests/undo-frames-replay.test.ts` fails 27 of 496 edges and `tests/undo-moves-replay.test.ts` 6 of 403 when every frame above is checked for a layer that does not show. `packages/doom/src/apply-edit.test.ts`, `packages/doom/src/invert.test.ts`, `packages/doom/src/invert-run.test.ts`, `packages/live/src/doom-undo-deleted-layer.test.ts`, `packages/live/src/doom-undo-steps.test.ts` and `tests/browser/editor-undo-deleted-layer.spec.ts` fail when a deleted or nested frame holds an undo or a redo, an undo deletes a frame with a layer in it, a deleted layer stays lost under a frame that comes back, or in a deleted frame that comes back, a layer left in place keeps a colour, words or size the step gave it, or a move that takes back no delete lands in a deleted frame.
