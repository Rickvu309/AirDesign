# A connector end paints where its shape last was, and delete stores that point as a free end

`lineRoute` in `packages/doom/src/line-ends.ts` is the only place an end becomes a painted point, and paint, layout findings and export all read it through `resolveSlide`. A hidden shape, or a shape on another slide, keeps its attachment and paints from the stored boxes of the chain the slide did not lay out. It never paints at the line's frame corner. `freedLineEnds` in `packages/doom/src/freed-line-ends.ts` runs before the parent clear in `compileDelete` (`packages/doom/src/agent-arrange.ts`) and in `deleteWrites` (`apps/web/src/editor/delete-writes.ts`), and writes the end it just painted as a free point. A shape that is already gone is left alone, because its last point is no longer on the slide. `packages/doom/src/line-end-place.test.ts` hides a shape, hides its frame, moves it to another slide, deletes it from both paths, deletes a frame around it, and undoes the delete. `tests/browser/editor-line-ends.spec.ts` deletes the shape from the keyboard and checks the saved end.
