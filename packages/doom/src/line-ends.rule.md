# A connector end paints where its shape last was, whichever person, agent, tab, undo or replay takes the shape off the deck

`lineRoute` in `packages/doom/src/line-ends.ts` is the only place an end becomes a painted point, and paint, layout findings and export all read it through `resolveSlide`. A hidden shape, or a shape on another slide, keeps its attachment. A hidden child of an auto-layout frame paints at the place the flow would give it, and that place does not push its shown siblings. A sibling that was already hidden stays out of the flow when a frame or a sibling is hidden. Anything else the slide did not lay out paints from the stored boxes of the chain, a deleted shape from its own box, and never at the line's frame corner. `freedLineEnds` in `packages/doom/src/freed-line-ends.ts` takes a batch of writes, applies it, and for every line end whose shape was on the deck before and is not after it, writes the point that end painted, carried through the placement of the line's frame before and after the batch, so a frame that turns or moves along a row keeps the point still. The write carries the base of the geometry it read. The rule reads what the writes did to the tree, not which gesture made them. A shape still on the deck, wherever it moved, keeps its attachment, and a line that is itself gone is left alone. Every edit that leaves a device runs it in one of two places. `pushEdit` in `packages/live/src/doom-deck.ts` runs it for gestures, text sessions, the filmstrip, undo, redo and run undo, with the measure the editor painted with (`measure` in `DoomOptions`, which `Editor` in `apps/web/src/editor/editor.tsx` keeps current). `compileAgentChange` (`packages/doom/src/agent-compile.ts`) runs it for each `edit_deck` change, and `undoRunTool` (`packages/mcp/src/undo-tool.ts`) for `undo_run`, with the host's measure of the fonts of the deck before and after the batch, taken whenever the batch may take a layer off the deck (`mayLeaveDeck` in `packages/doom/src/freed-line-ends.ts`). The other tab's writes are reconciled in the core. A freed end read before another tab changed the line is refused `stale` with the whole edit, so neither tab's line is lost. A write that names a shape off the deck keeps the end the line already holds, if the line stays in its frame (`keepLiveEnds` in `packages/doom/src/apply-edit.ts`), and `seedsOf` in `apps/api/src/doom/doom-deck.ts` loads the named shapes so the server's partial fold judges the same. A tab that never saw the line leaves its end on the deleted shape, which paints from the shape's box. `invert` and `invertRun` put back each end that still holds what the step wrote, even after another tab changed the other end (`lineEndsBack` in `packages/doom/src/line-ends-back.ts`), so undo restores the attachment. While a text that will leave the deck is empty in the editor, `draftWrites` in `apps/web/src/editor/text-leave.ts` previews its freed end, so leaving the editor keeps the point the person saw. `packages/doom/src/line-end-place.test.ts` and `packages/doom/src/freed-line-ends.test.ts` cover hiding, other slides, delete, frames, ungroup, unwrap, slide delete, measured widths, a frame moved by a row and a turned frame. `packages/doom/src/line-end-writes.test.ts` covers a write naming a deleted shape, per-end undo and run undo, and paint after an unseen delete. `packages/live/src/doom-line-ends-tabs.test.ts` replays two tabs: a stale move, an undo that removes another tab's shape, an unseen delete and a stale delete. `apps/web/src/editor/line-end-gestures.test.ts` pushes emptying a text, delete, ungroup and slide delete through the deck with undo and redo, and the empty text preview. `packages/mcp/src/edit-tool.test.ts` runs `unwrap`, a removed font and slide delete through `edit_deck`, and `undo_run` of another run's shape. `apps/api/src/doom-deck-line-ends.test.ts` lands an attach and a stale write through the server. `tests/browser/editor-line-ends.spec.ts` deletes the shape from the keyboard and checks the saved end.
