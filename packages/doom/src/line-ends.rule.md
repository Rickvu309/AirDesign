# A connector end paints where its shape last was, and delete stores that point as a free end

`lineRoute` in `packages/doom/src/line-ends.ts` is the only place an end becomes a painted point, and paint, layout findings and export all read it through `resolveSlide`. A hidden shape, or a shape on another slide, keeps its attachment. A hidden child of an auto-layout frame paints at the place the flow would give it, and that place does not push its shown siblings. Anything else the slide did not lay out paints from the stored boxes of the chain. It never paints at the line's frame corner. `freedLineEnds` in `packages/doom/src/freed-line-ends.ts` runs before the parent clear in `compileDelete` (`packages/doom/src/agent-arrange.ts`) and in `deleteWrites` (`apps/web/src/editor/delete-writes.ts`), and writes the end it just painted as a free point. The editor passes the measure `useDoomSlide` painted with. The agent passes the measure `host.measure` gave layout findings (`deckMeasure` in `packages/mcp/src/edit-tool.ts`). The empty `measureWith({})` is an estimate, and a freed end that used it is exact only when the shape's place does not depend on text width. A shape that is already gone is left alone, because its last point is no longer on the slide. `packages/doom/src/line-end-place.test.ts` hides a shape, hides its frame, moves it to another slide, deletes it from both paths with a width-scaled measure, deletes a frame around it, paints a hidden auto-layout child at its flow position, and undoes the delete. `apps/web/src/editor/delete-line-end.test.ts` checks the editor writes that same point. `tests/browser/editor-line-ends.spec.ts` deletes the shape from the keyboard and checks the saved end.
