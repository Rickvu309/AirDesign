# A pattern picks one text step by what fits the band: a KPI row's number, a grid's cells, a flow's direction and a timeline's direction, and never splits a word

`packages/doom/src/agent-patterns.test.ts`, `packages/doom/src/agent-grid.test.ts` and `packages/doom/src/agent-flow.test.ts` hold the fits below, each the largest step at which the words fit, one step for every cell, and no agent field to override it.

KPI row, in `agent-patterns.test.ts`: it builds two KPI slides whose values are the same width, one of them with a wrapped note, and fails unless every number is bigNumber. It builds a 4:3 slide with `$1,234,567.89`, `$987,654.32` and `$246,913.57` and fails unless the three numbers share the largest style whose widest line fits the card, and that style is not bigNumber.

Grid of words, in `agent-grid.test.ts`: it builds a four row grid and fails unless every cell, header included, is heading, the largest step the grid uses. It builds an eleven row grid and fails unless every cell shares one smaller style and the grid ends above the bottom margin. It fails unless the header row and the first column are semibold and every other cell is regular, in both grids. It fails unless every cell is set in the theme's body font, so a heading font that is one named weight such as "Inter SemiBold" cannot paint values semibold. It builds a four-column grid of long words on a 4:3 slide and fails if any word, a bold one counted wider, is wider than its column, and it fails unless a word no step fits is refused with "shorter labels, fewer columns or fewer rows". It fails unless the rows stay at the height of their words, the theme gap apart, from the top of the band.

Flow, in `agent-flow.test.ts`: the order is across at the normal text step if every label fits on one line, else down at that step if the stack fits the band, else across at the largest smaller step where the longest word of every card fits, else refused. It builds four short steps and fails unless the lane is a row, each arrow running from a step's right side to the next one's left side. It builds four steps with one long label and three long steps and fails unless the lane is a column in heading. It builds the same five steps on a 16:9 and a 4:3 slide and fails unless the first runs across and the second down. It builds six long steps at 16:9 and six short steps at 16:9 and 4:3 and fails if any word is wider than its card. It fails unless six long steps on a 4:3 slide, and a word no step fits, are refused with "shorter labels or fewer steps".

Timeline, in `agent-flow.test.ts`: the order is across at the normal text step if every whole word fits, else down, else across at the largest smaller step where every whole word fits, else refused. It builds three steps under a title and fails unless the rail starts at the title's left edge and ends at its right edge. It fails unless a timeline with an explicit box keeps that box's corner and width. It builds 3, 6 and 8 steps on 16:9 and on 4:3 and fails unless each runs across, in whole words, inside the bottom margin, with three steps at heading. It builds a word that does not fit a three-step row and fails unless the timeline runs down at heading.
