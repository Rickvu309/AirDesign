# An undo puts a layer back before it writes to it and deletes a layer after, so a step never refuses its own writes

`invert` in `packages/doom/src/invert.ts` writes the moves that put a layer back first, by depth, then the other writes, then every write of `parent: null`. The deck refuses a write to a deleted layer as `missing` and refuses the whole edit with it, and `turn` in `packages/live/src/doom-deck.ts` keeps a refused step on top. When deletes went first with the other moves, the redo of a text box emptied and left (typing and its delete in one step) deleted the layer before writing its text, so every Ctrl+Shift+Z was refused for good (verdict 1507-1fb8aa5, finding 1). `NeverStuck` in `specs/undo-steps/UndoSteps.tla` fails in 82 states with deletes first and in 28 with moves last, `tests/undo-steps-replay.test.ts` disagrees on 14 and 54 of 512 edges, and `packages/doom/src/invert.test.ts`, `packages/live/src/doom-undo-deleted-layer.test.ts` and `tests/browser/doom-undo-deleted-layer.spec.ts` fail when a step that deletes its own layer cannot be redone.
