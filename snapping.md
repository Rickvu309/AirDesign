# Snapping

How a layer lines up while a person drags it on the slide.

## Language

**snapping**:
While a layer is moved or resized by pointer, its edges and centre jump onto the edges and centre of another layer or of the slide once they come within 4.5 screen pixels, at every zoom. Moving also snaps to an equal gap in the layer's row or column. Holding Control during the drag turns it off. Arrow keys never snap.
_Avoid_: magnet, stick, smart snapping

**snap guide**:
The red line drawn while a layer is held, through every edge or centre it lines up with, with a small cross at each lined-up point. It goes when the layer is let go.
_Avoid_: smart guide, alignment line, guide (a guide is a ruler line a person places, which AirDesign does not have)

**spacing guide**:
The pink marks drawn across two or more equal gaps in a row or column while a layer is held, each with the gap in points.
_Avoid_: distribution marker, equal spacing guide
