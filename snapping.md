# Snapping

How a layer lines up while a person drags it on the slide.

## Language

**snapping**:
While a layer is moved or resized by pointer, its edges and centre jump onto the edges and centre of another layer or of the slide, or onto a shown guide, once they come within 4.5 screen pixels, at every zoom. Moving also snaps to an equal gap in the layer's row or column. A resize snaps only the edges that move: from the centre with Alt, with Shift, by an edge, on a rotated layer (by its outermost points) and on a multi-selection (by the edges around all of it). Holding Control during the drag turns it off. Arrow keys never snap.
_Avoid_: magnet, stick, smart snapping

**snap guide**:
The red line drawn while a layer is held, through every edge or centre it lines up with, with a small cross at each lined-up point. It goes when the layer is let go.
_Avoid_: smart guide, alignment line, guide (a guide is a line a person pulls out of a ruler; see `rulers.md`)

**spacing guide**:
The pink marks drawn across two or more equal gaps in a row or column while a layer is held, each with the gap in points.
_Avoid_: distribution marker, equal spacing guide

**measuring**:
Holding Alt (Option on a Mac) with a layer selected shows red lines and labels, in points, for the distances from it to the layer under the pointer, or to the slide's edges when the pointer is on no other layer. The measured layer or the slide is outlined in red.
_Avoid_: redlines, measure tool (Figma's saved measurements are another thing)
