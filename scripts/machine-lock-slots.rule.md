# Full checks take one slot per 16 cores and 12 GB of memory, a slot past the first only while the load average is below the core count; a file from a failed `unit-tests` step can rerun alone without waiting behind them

`takeMachineLock` in `scripts/machine-lock.mjs` keeps two lanes: `full`, with the slots of `checkSlots`, for `pnpm check` and `suites --run`, and `small` for land's alone rerun, which runs niced on two workers (`rerun` in `scripts/land.mjs`). Each queue row names its lane and status; `scripts/machine-lock.test.mjs` runs a small job beside a full check, queues two small jobs, runs two full checks in two slots while a third waits, and keeps the second slot shut while the load is above the core count
