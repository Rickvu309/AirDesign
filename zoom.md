# Zoom

How a person moves around the canvas: how near the slide looks and which part of it shows. Figma Slides' words.

## Language

**zoom**:
How large the slide shows, in percent of its size at 96 pixels an inch. Slide view zooms from 10% to 400% and opens at zoom to fit. Ctrl or Command with the scroll wheel, or a trackpad pinch, zooms about the cursor; keys and the zoom menu zoom about the middle of the canvas.
_Avoid_: scale, magnification

**zoom to fit**:
The zoom that shows the whole slide above the toolbar. Shift+1.
_Avoid_: fit to screen, reset zoom

**zoom to selection**:
The zoom that fills the canvas with the selected layers, 48 pixels in from its edges, and centres them. Shift+2.
_Avoid_: focus, zoom to layer

**zoom to 100%**:
The zoom at the slide's own size. Shift+0, or Ctrl or Command with 0.
_Avoid_: actual size, reset zoom

**pan**:
To move which part of a zoomed slide shows, without moving anything on it: scroll, Shift with the scroll wheel, a drag while Space is held, or a drag with the hand tool (H).
_Avoid_: scroll (for the gesture), drag the canvas
