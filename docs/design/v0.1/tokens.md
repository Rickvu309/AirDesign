# Tokens

The palette follows paper.design: neutral grays and one blue. Orange and other warm tints are not used. Red is only for errors, limits and deletes.

## Type

Geist. Sentence case everywhere.

| Role | Size / weight |
|---|---|
| Page title | 26 semibold, -0.5 tracking |
| Dialog title | 17 semibold |
| Module title | 15 semibold |
| Body | 13 to 14 regular, line height 1.5 |
| Helper and notes | 12 regular, `gray.600` or `gray.500` |
| Button | 13 to 14 medium |

## Radius

Dialogs 16. Composer 14. Module cards, notice cards and alert icon tiles 12. Toasts 10. Buttons, inputs, selects and banners 8. Chips and small logo tiles 6 to 7.

## Elevation

- Dialog: 1px 8% black ring, 0 24 48 at 16%.
- Primary button: white inner highlight at 35%, 1px `brand.strong` edge, 0 1 2 at 12%.
- Toast: 0 8 24 at 20%.
- Working glow: 0 0 28 `brand` at 35%, plus offset blue and violet glows at 28%.

## Spacing

Dialog padding 24, gap 16 to 20. Module card padding 20, gap 14, 16 between cards. Settings body 860 wide. Screens 1470 x 923.
