# AirDesign landing-page research

Research date: 4 November 2026. Scope: 16 live landing pages verified through web search and first-party page fetches. These are **current 2026 references**, not claims that each design launched in 2025–2026. No archived 2025 version was inspected. Page text, product-demo markup, image descriptions and, where available, downloaded HTML/CSS inform the observations. Exact rendered geometry, animation timing and interaction behavior were not browser-tested. A demo described below is a marketing demonstration, not proof of a live backend. Recommendations are original adaptations, not claims about existing AirDesign features beyond the supplied brief.

## Direction

Make a consulting-quality slide the main visual, with Aira's work visible beside it. Demonstrate the progression from business evidence to recommendation to editable deck. The strongest reference combination is **Framer's editable canvas + Granola's transformation + Linear's reviewable agent activity + Rogo/Hebbia's professional specificity + Plus AI's PowerPoint reassurance**. Use Vercel for typographic discipline, not infrastructure vocabulary.

AirDesign's advantage must be apparent in the content of the example: a defensible action title, an analytical chart, and a clear recommendation. A beautiful title slide alone cannot demonstrate consulting-grade work.

## Ten best concrete ideas for AirDesign

Static labels: **Yes** = persuasive in a single design frame; **Paired** = needs two or three static frames to communicate the change; **Partial** = a design file can specify it, but implementation is required to demonstrate the behavior. All sample figures and business names below are illustrative and must be labeled as such.

### 1. Hero: one decision, one slide, Aira visibly working

- **Composition:** Short headline above a wide product frame. Aira occupies approximately one quarter of its width; a readable 16:9 slide occupies the rest. Include a small thumbnail rail only if the active slide remains legible.
- **Example:** Request: “Turn this operating update into a board recommendation.” Aira shows the story outline, writes an action title, then builds the exhibit. End on a finished slide and a review state.
- **Identity:** Paper-gray canvas, white slide, charcoal text; blue spark identifies Aira. The blue-to-violet working glow belongs only to the active slide edge. It settles when work completes.
- **Static:** **Yes** for the final frame with completed activity steps; **Paired** for the sequence. Sources: [Framer](https://www.framer.com/), [Cursor](https://cursor.com/), [Linear](https://linear.app/).

### 2. Make an action-title rewrite the signature moment

- **Content:** Start with “Regional performance.” Replace it with “Prioritize North America: it contributed 60% of incremental revenue.” Underneath, show an illustrative table where $6m of the $10m total revenue increase comes from North America.
- **Interaction:** Select the old title; reveal the replacement as a whole phrase. A brief highlight connects the claim to the exhibit. Avoid letter-by-letter theatrical typing.
- **Why:** This demonstrates the difference between decorating information and framing a decision. The claim must follow from supplied evidence; it must not imply unverified causation.
- **Static:** **Paired**, using before/after title strips above the same chart. Source principle: [Granola](https://www.granola.ai/).

### 3. A PowerPoint before/after with identical facts

- **Content:** Show the same slide before and after: identical values, improved argument, hierarchy and chart labeling. Default to “After.” Include a short list of what changed.
- **Interaction:** A discrete Before/After switch is the primary control. An optional wipe handle can reveal layout differences, but must not be the only way to compare or read the title.
- **Proof:** Provide both downloadable sample files once built. Use one executive analytics slide, not an ugly caricature compared with an unrelated beautiful deck.
- **Static:** **Paired**; side-by-side comparison works in a design file. Sources: [Plus AI](https://plusai.com/), [Beautiful.ai](https://www.beautiful.ai/).

### 4. Aira's activity feed names observable work

- **Content:** Four concise steps: read the input deck; frame the recommendation; write action titles; build the slides. Link each represented step visually to the relevant artifact. The current task may identify a specific slide.
- **Interaction:** Completed items collapse into compact checkmarks; only the current step receives emphasis. Provide replay for a scripted demo, labeled “Example workflow.” Do not expose invented internal reasoning or pretend a recording is running live.
- **Identity:** Blue spark on the current task, neutral completed tasks, a clear end state. The glow marks the slide receiving work.
- **Static:** **Yes** with two completed steps and one current task. Sources: [Rogo](https://rogo.com/), [Linear](https://linear.app/), [Attio](https://attio.com/).

### 5. Prove PowerPoint editability with an actual object

- **Section:** Follow “PowerPoint in. Editable PowerPoint out.” with three artifacts: input .pptx, AirDesign slide, exported PowerPoint open with a native object selected.
- **Proof:** Show editable title text and a native chart or shape where the real export supports it. Attach the actual sample .pptx. A selection box drawn over a flattened image proves nothing.
- **Interaction:** A short user-triggered clip can edit the exported title and move a shape. Be precise about which elements remain editable; avoid claiming perfect fidelity before verification.
- **Static:** **Yes** for the selected-object frame; **Partial** for proving actual editing. Sources: [Plus AI](https://plusai.com/), [Gamma](https://gamma.app/).

### 6. Show the story spine before the slide gallery

- **Section:** A five-slide outline for an illustrative board update: decision, evidence, options, recommendation and next steps. Write every item as a real action title. Selecting a title reveals its corresponding finished slide.
- **Why:** The reader can inspect the argument across the deck, not only admire disconnected templates. Use one consistent case so assumptions and figures remain coherent.
- **Static:** **Yes**: left-side numbered story spine, one expanded exhibit at right. Also works as a five-panel strip in a wide design file.
- **Source principles:** [Gamma](https://gamma.app/), [Beautiful.ai](https://www.beautiful.ai/). This is an AirDesign proposal, not a claim that those sites use this exact layout.

### 7. A “red pen” review of one finished slide

- **Section:** Annotate three specific improvements: topic title becomes recommendation; unlabeled chart becomes an interpretable exhibit; unsupported assertion is removed or qualified. Anchor each note to the exact element.
- **Identity:** Use numbered charcoal annotations with blue focus outlines. “Red pen” describes editorial scrutiny; red need not become another brand accent. Keep the logo's pink stroke for the logo.
- **Proof:** Present this as an annotated example of the quality standard. Do not advertise an automated review/scoring feature unless one exists.
- **Static:** **Yes**, especially strong as a design-file centerpiece. Source principle: evidence specificity in [Hebbia](https://www.hebbia.com/).

### 8. One deck, three ways to direct the work

- **Section:** Aira / Claude / Codex tabs share one slide canvas. Aira is selected by default. Each tab pairs the same executive request with the same deck outcome; Claude and Codex are identified as connections over MCP.
- **Interaction:** Switching tabs changes the instruction surface while preserving the artifact. Link to connection instructions below the example; show verified setup steps rather than an invented one-click connection.
- **Why:** Establishes that executives can start with the built-in agent and connected-agent users can work from their preferred environment. MCP is a route to the outcome, not the category headline.
- **Static:** **Yes** for the default and one alternative frame. Sources: [Attio](https://attio.com/), [Framer](https://www.framer.com/), [Vercel](https://vercel.com/).

### 9. Proof as a case file, not a logo wall

- **Section:** One genuine executive case: role and organization, input material, resulting deck, what the person changed, and an approved quote. Show two or three real slides, or an explicitly labeled anonymized version.
- **Metrics:** If presenting time saved, state the task scope and whether the time includes review/revisions. If no measured result exists, let the downloadable output carry the proof. Do not invent a quantified benefit or a consulting-firm endorsement.
- **Static:** **Yes**. A finished deck, attribution and concise methodology fit in one card.
- **Sources:** [Harvey](https://www.harvey.ai/), [Mercury](https://mercury.com/), [Rogo](https://rogo.com/).

### 10. A calm free-start close with a precise visual system

- **Section:** Repeat the principal CTA after the example and compatibility proof. Explain that access is free with weekly usage. Publish the actual allowance and reset terms when confirmed; the brief provides no number. Offer “View example deck” as the secondary route.
- **Micro-details:** Geist throughout; tabular figures in exhibits; quiet 1px rules; aligned slide numbers and file labels; restrained corner rounding. AirDesign's A carries its pink stroke; Aira uses the blue open-A/star mark.
- **Button:** Soft #93B8EF → #4D94FB gradient, 1px #2D6FCD edge and inner highlight. Use a dark label if contrast requires it. Keep broad surfaces #F5F5F2 to white, text near #222; reserve blue/violet glow for active work.
- **Static:** **Yes** for the CTA and full visual system; **Partial** for hover/focus and glow transitions. Source principles: [Vercel](https://vercel.com/), [Mercury](https://mercury.com/). Colors and marks come from the supplied AirDesign identity.

## Suggested section order

1. Executive outcome + Aira working on a readable slide; free-start CTA and PowerPoint reassurance.
2. Action-title transformation, using the same evidence as the hero.
3. Five-slide story spine with inspectable exhibits.
4. Before/after and editable PowerPoint proof, including sample download.
5. Annotated quality standard and a real executive case when available.
6. Aira / Claude / Codex routes to the same deck.
7. Weekly free-usage terms, focused FAQ and final CTA.

## Anti-patterns for this audience

- **A giant empty prompt field as the entire hero.** It asks the executive to supply the vision before demonstrating any judgment. Show a finished recommendation and one grounded request.
- **A generic “AI presentation maker” promise.** The example needs to demonstrate executive narrative and analytical structure, not just instant slide production.
- **A beautiful cover slide as the sole proof.** Use a bridge, comparison, operating-metrics exhibit or decision slide that shows how the product handles substantive work.
- **Tiny, tilted, floating screenshots.** If the action title and chart labels cannot be read at normal size, the central claim cannot be assessed.
- **Changing the evidence during a before/after.** Improve the reasoning and presentation while retaining the facts; distinguish supplied facts from proposed interpretation.
- **A fake live run.** Scripted activity is useful when labeled. Do not imply a demo is processing an upload, checking sources or measuring quality when it is not.
- **Endless agent thought streams.** Show task, visible change and review state. Executives need confidence in the output, not a performance of internal reasoning.
- **Permanent working glow, ambient gradient clouds and pulsing controls.** Active-work styling must mean something. Keep the page quiet when no work is happening.
- **A decorative bot mascot or oversized sparkles.** Aira's mark can identify authorship and state without making the professional workflow feel playful.
- **Developer-first messaging.** MCP, terminals and tool schemas belong after the executive value and Aira demonstration; use actual connected workflows rather than infrastructure diagrams.
- **Unverifiable authority.** Do not use McKinsey/BCG/Bain logos, fabricated executive quotes or “trusted by” claims to imply endorsement. Explain the intended standard through the work.
- **Unqualified speed and accuracy claims.** Separate draft generation from human review and ground any claimed saving in an attributable case.
- **Export relegated to an FAQ.** Compatibility and editability answer a central adoption concern. Demonstrate them visibly and provide a real file.
- **A “free” CTA that conceals the weekly allowance.** Show the confirmed usage terms near signup. Do not turn weekly free access into “unlimited,” a trial or an enterprise-demo funnel.
- **Motion required to understand the page.** Completed static states, reduced-motion presentation, readable mobile slides, keyboard-operable comparisons and user-triggered replay must preserve the argument.
- **Fashionable low-contrast gray or white labels on pale blue.** Premium restraint still requires readable copy, chart labels, controls and focus states.
