The Images dock opens the left picker from S43.03. Personal images, free local stock and MIT icons share that picker. Source and license remain visible in each record. Upload and clipboard paste run asynchronously through the isolated validator; cancellation leaves the deck unchanged.

A blank library offers upload or paste. A stock search keeps its query when no results are found and offers other words or upload. Refused files show the reason and retain their original outside the application. Storage full offers reuse of existing library images; it never deletes placed assets. Removing a listing keeps the original available to authorized decks.

The placement editor previews crop and focal changes locally while the person drags. Save creates one undoable person command. Crop, focal point, rectangle or ellipse mask and alt text belong to this placement. A low-resolution warning allows continued use. Saving never changes the immutable original or another placement. Source bytes remain owned even after removal from the visible library.
