# Effects

A layer's ordered list of Drop shadow, Inner shadow, glow, soft edge and blur. Effects keeps exact settings. A group's effects paint around its contents.

A layer takes up to four Drop shadows and four Inner shadows. A title or body takes one Drop shadow.

The Shadow preset has three strengths, `subtle`, `medium` and `strong` in code, which the panel names Smaller, Middle and Larger beside a shadow opacity slider.
