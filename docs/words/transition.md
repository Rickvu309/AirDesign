# Transition

How a slide arrives when presenting.

## Language

**transition**:
How a slide arrives when presenting: its style, none, fade, push or Smart animate, and how long that style takes. It is the slide's `transition` prop.
_Avoid_: animation, effect

**Smart animate**:
The transition style `smartMove`: it moves, resizes and turns each layer the two slides share, and fades the rest.
_Avoid_: morph, magic move
