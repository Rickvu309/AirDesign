# All journeys

Every journey in v0.1, grouped by the five stages a person moves through.

15 journeys. Each one gets its own board with steps and screens when it is mapped.

Legend: Trunk, everyone. Must for v0.1. Later.

## 1 Discover

- **F10**: Mapped. Learn before signing up. Landing, pricing, agents guide.
- **F01**: Mapped. Sign in and account. Email link and code, intent kept. Google and Microsoft later.

## 2 First deck

### Start a deck, one per way in

- **F02**: Mapped. First deck. Describe it, or ask your own agent. I have an idea.
- **F05**: Mapped. From a PowerPoint. Import, report, restyle. I have a PowerPoint.
- **F06**: Mapped. Connect a User Agent. Add the link, sign in, approve. I use my own agent.

### Shape it, everyone

- **F03**: Mapped. Edit and refine. Ask the agent, edit by hand, undo a run.
- **F11**: Mapped. Brand and themes. Pick a theme, or build one from a PowerPoint.
- **F12**: Mapped. Images and assets. Upload, place, crop, reuse.

## 3 Take it out

- **F04**: Mapped. Export to PowerPoint. PowerPoint that stays editable, plus PDF and PNG.
- **F13**: Later, mapped. Share a view link. Read-only link, revoke any time.

## 4 Come back

- **F08**: Mapped. Next deck. Starts from their own style.
- **F14**: Mapped. Manage decks. Find, rename, duplicate, delete.

## 5 Grow

- **F07**: Mapped. Credits and plan. Notice, upgrade, top up.
- **F09**: Mapped. Settings and data. Profile, agents, export, delete.

## Across every stage

- **F15**: When something goes wrong. Agent run fails, import refused, export fails, sign-in link expires. Each says what happened, what is safe, and one next step. Mapped.
