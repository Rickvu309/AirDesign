# Structure check

Same fields as [checks-document.md](checks-document.md). The replay runs with the others: `pnpm vitest run tests/spec-replay.test.ts`.

## structure

Adding and removing an element, a run's read of the element list, grouping and ungrouping elements, and auto layout frames.

- `StructuralRecord`: an add or remove changes only the named element.
- `ReadSetHeld`: a read keeps the element list it saw.
- `AbsentEditLeavesCounts`: an edit naming an absent element changes no counts.
- `ChildrenKeepPlace`: a child of a group shows where the person last saw it through group, a move of the group, ungroup, the person's Undo and Redo of each, and a run's Undo of a group the agent made. For an auto layout frame it also holds through Shift+A, a padding change (the one move the person means), Remove auto layout and its Undo, a move of the child inside a frame without auto layout, a drag of the child out of its frame and the Undo that puts it back, ungroup of a frame with or without auto layout and the Undo of that, and the Undo of the wrap.
- `BackdropOnlyInFrame`: a frame's backdrop is on the slide exactly while its frame is formed.
- `LeftChildStaysOut`: a child that left its frame is outside it only while the frame exists, so an undone wrap or ungroup leaves it where it was.
- `RunUndoTurnsBackGroup`: a run's Undo of a group the agent made leaves it ungrouped for good.
- `PersonMoveKeepsAgentGroup`: a run's Undo refuses to ungroup the agent's group once the person moved it, or once a child's own rows no longer match where the group command placed it.

Bounds: two elements, `a` always present; one group or frame with one child the model follows, the frame's padding at none or one step, moved by at most one step, the child moved by at most one step on the slide. The group sub-model runs from one quiet start (no record, no read, idle run `s`), and the node actions run only while no group exists, so the two parts share no states. Run: `pnpm check:specs structure`. Replay: `tests/structure-replay.ts`. `replayNodes` drives `decide` `add`, `remove` and `read`; `replayTree` reaches every group state from the quiet start through the code (`groupOf`, `ungroupTurn`, `regroupTurn`, person box rows on the group and the child, `undoSlides`, and for frames `changeLayout` wrap and remove, a person's padding row, a person's box row on the child of a frame without auto layout, `changeLayout` place for a drop outside the frame and `undoOf` of its `slideUndoable`, `changeLayout` move for a person's layers-panel drag of the child out of a plain group and `undoOf` of its join step, `undoOf` of the Remove step and `ungroupTurn` with `"made"` for the wrap's Undo) and checks each edge's shown place (`placeOf`, through `throughGroup` while grouped, `readLayout` while framed), `groupFormed`, `groupedNow`, whether the backdrop is gone and whether auto layout is on. Code: `packages/document/src/element-group.ts` (the folded group tree, and the frame a plain group fits to after a join), `group-refusal.ts`, `group-join.ts` (`joinTurn`, `spotNow`), `group-space.ts`, `group-place.ts`, `group-turn.ts`, `readLayout`, `changeLayout`, `refrozenRows`, `frameNow` and `framedNow` in `frame-layout.ts`, `stepCommands` (a Remove step undoes its children before its frame), the group branch of `slideUndoable`, `turn` and `turnCommands` (a person's Undo and Redo of a group) and of `undoSlides` with `groupUndone` (a run's Undo of a group, and the refusal `PersonMoveKeepsAgentGroup` checks) in `person-undo.ts`. Leaves out: product code adds a register with `add` (`openedRegister` in `call.ts`, `opened` in `packages/live/src/cache.ts`) and uses `remove` only to put a restored register back (`decideOnElement`). `read` has no product caller. A person's element add and remove go through slide commands, covered by element. Rotation, flips and scale of a group are not modelled (a join into a scaled group is tested in `packages/document/src/group-join.test.ts` and `packages/pptx/src/group-join-route.test.ts`; a turned or flipped group refuses joins); `throughGroup` is tested against the painter in `packages/document/src/element-group.test.ts` and against the export in `packages/pptx/src/group-route.test.ts`. The export's replay of group commands (`groupsReplayed` in `packages/pptx/src/group-route.ts`) is tested there, not modelled. An agent's own move of its group, then a person's ungroup and Undo of it, is refused by `rowsKept` and tested in `element-group.test.ts`. Frames: the model follows the first child of a row, so a gap change, which moves only later children, is tested in `packages/document/src/frame-layout.test.ts`; frames are made by a person only. A frame read from a PowerPoint file (its tag read by `layoutFromFile` into the index's `framing`) folds through the same `frameSource`; it is tested in `packages/document/src/frame-filed.test.ts` and `packages/pptx/src/frame-route.test.ts`, not modelled. Shift+A on a frame without auto layout lays its children out again, a move the person means, so it is tested in `frame-layout.test.ts` and `tests/browser/auto-layout-frame.spec.ts`, not modelled.
