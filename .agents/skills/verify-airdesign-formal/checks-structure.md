# Structure check

Same fields as [checks-document.md](checks-document.md). The replay runs with the others: `pnpm vitest run tests/spec-replay.test.ts`.

## structure

Adding and removing an element, a run's read of the element list, grouping and ungrouping elements, and auto layout frames.

- `StructuralRecord`: an add or remove changes only the named element.
- `ReadSetHeld`: a read keeps the element list it saw.
- `AbsentEditLeavesCounts`: an edit naming an absent element changes no counts.
- `ChildrenKeepPlace`: a child of a group shows where the person last saw it through group, a move of the group, a move of the child inside the group and its Undo, ungroup, the person's Undo and Redo of each, and a run's Undo of a group the agent made. For an auto layout frame it also holds through Shift+A, a padding change (the one move the person means), Remove auto layout and its Undo, a move of the child inside a frame without auto layout, a drag of the child out of its frame and the Undo that puts it back, a drag of the child out of its plain group, then of the group's other layer, which deletes the emptied group, and the Undo of each, ungroup of a frame with or without auto layout and the Undo of that, and the Undo of the wrap.
- `BackdropOnlyInFrame`: a frame's backdrop is on the slide exactly while its frame is formed.
- `EmptiedGroupHoldsNoLayer`: a group deletes itself only once the child is out of it, and it is never a frame.
- `LeftChildStaysOut`: a child that left its frame is outside it only while the frame exists, so an undone wrap or ungroup leaves it where it was.
- `RunUndoTurnsBackGroup`: a run's Undo of a group the agent made leaves it ungrouped for good.
- `PersonMoveKeepsAgentGroup`: a run's Undo refuses to ungroup the agent's group once the person moved it, or once a child's own rows no longer match where the group command placed it.

Bounds: two elements, `a` always present; one group or frame with one child the model follows, the frame's padding at none or one step, moved by at most one step, the child moved by at most one step on the slide while no group holds it (not after its group emptied, to stay under 500 states), or by one step inside a group the person made before the group moves. The group sub-model runs from one quiet start (no record, no read, idle run `s`), and the node actions run only while no group exists, so the two parts share no states. Run: `pnpm check:specs structure`. Replay: `tests/structure-replay.ts`. `replayNodes` drives `decide` `add`, `remove` and `read`; `replayTree` reaches every group state from the quiet start through the code (`groupOf`, `ungroupTurn`, `regroupTurn`, person box rows on the group and the child, a move inside the group planned by `planChange` from the child's `shownPlace`, `undoSlides`, and for frames `changeLayout` wrap and remove, a person's padding row, a person's box row on the child of a frame without auto layout, `changeLayout` place for a drop outside the frame and `undoOf` of its `slideUndoable`, `changeLayout` move for a person's layers-panel drag of the child out of a plain group and `undoOf` of its join step, the same for the group's other layer, whose turn ends with `element-remove` of the emptied group and whose Undo starts with `element-restore`, `undoOf` of the Remove step and `ungroupTurn` with `"made"` for the wrap's Undo) and checks each edge's shown place (`placeOf`, through `throughGroup` while grouped, `readLayout` while framed), `groupFormed`, `groupedNow`, whether the backdrop is gone and whether auto layout is on. Code: `packages/document/src/element-group.ts` (the folded group tree, and the groups a join entered or left, a copy joined, or a copy made), `group-refusal.ts`, `group-join.ts` (`joinTurn`, `joinEdit`, `spotNow`, `emptiedGroups`, `removedGroups`), `elementRefusal` in `element-order.ts` (the remove of an emptied group), `group-space.ts` (a grouped layer's place on the slide and back, the bounds a group fits to after a move inside it or a layer joins or leaves it, and `regroupedRows` on Undo of an ungroup), `group-place.ts`, `group-turn.ts`, `readLayout`, `changeLayout`, `refrozenRows`, `frameNow` and `framedNow` in `frame-layout.ts`, `stepCommands` (a Remove step undoes its children before its frame), the group branch of `slideUndoable`, `turn` and `turnCommands` (a person's Undo and Redo of a group) and of `undoSlides` with `groupUndone` (a run's Undo of a group, and the refusal `PersonMoveKeepsAgentGroup` checks) in `person-undo.ts`. Leaves out: product code adds a register with `add` (`openedRegister` in `call.ts`, `opened` in `packages/live/src/cache.ts`) and uses `remove` only to put a restored register back (`decideOnElement`). `read` has no product caller. A person's element add and remove go through slide commands, covered by element. Rotation, flips and scale of a group are not modelled: a join into or out of a scaled, turned, flipped or nested group, a frame joining a group, an emptied nested group and every emptied group above it, are tested in `packages/document/src/group-join.test.ts`, `group-join-space.test.ts`, `packages/pptx/src/group-join-route.test.ts` and `group-join-nested.test.ts`; `throughGroup` is tested against the painter in `packages/document/src/element-group.test.ts` and against the export in `packages/pptx/src/group-route.test.ts`. The export's replay of group commands (`groupsReplayed` in `packages/pptx/src/group-route.ts`) is tested there, not modelled. An agent's own move of its group, then a person's ungroup and Undo of it, is refused by `rowsKept` and tested in `element-group.test.ts`; a run's Undo of an agent's group whose child the person moved inside is refused the same way and tested in `group-space.test.ts`, kept out of the model to stay under 500 states. The replay's groups are made by AirDesign, so their child space is the slide's: the conversion through a scaled, turned, flipped or nested group, the bounds that follow the children, and the rows `regroupedRows` writes are tested in `packages/document/src/group-space.test.ts` and `packages/pptx/src/group-follow.test.ts`. A layer inside a group inside a frame on the slide goes through the frame's laid box for its group (`laidPlace` in `group-space.ts`), and the frame lays out a group that fits its moved layers at its fitted box, or at its fitted size with auto layout (`readLayout`'s settled boxes); tested in `packages/document/src/group-in-frame.test.ts`, `packages/pptx/src/group-in-frame.test.ts` and `tests/browser/frame-group-move.spec.ts`, not modelled. Frames: the model follows the first child of a row, so a gap change, which moves only later children, is tested in `packages/document/src/frame-layout.test.ts`; frames are made by a person only. A frame read from a PowerPoint file (its tag read by `layoutFromFile` into the index's `framing`) folds through the same `frameSource`; it is tested in `packages/document/src/frame-filed.test.ts` and `packages/pptx/src/frame-route.test.ts`, not modelled. Shift+A on a frame without auto layout lays its children out again, a move the person means, so it is tested in `frame-layout.test.ts` and `tests/browser/auto-layout-frame.spec.ts`, not modelled. A frame inside an auto layout frame (Shift+A on a selection holding a frame, a frame dropped on a frame, the inner frame laid out at the outer frame's slot and at the size it hugs, Remove auto layout and Ungroup of either, a frame refused into itself or a frame inside it) is tested in `packages/document/src/frame-nested.test.ts`, `packages/pptx/src/frame-route.test.ts`, `packages/pptx/src/frame-fade.test.ts` and `packages/engine/src/agent-frames.test.ts`, not modelled.
