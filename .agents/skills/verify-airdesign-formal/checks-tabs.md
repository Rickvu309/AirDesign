# Checks on two tabs sharing one deck

Same fields as [checks-document.md](checks-document.md). tabs is replayed in `packages/live/src/tabs-replay.test.ts`, which runs two copies of `packages/live` on one IndexedDB: `pnpm vitest run packages/live/src/tabs-replay.test.ts`. One tab's lost answer, its reloads and its mirror are lost-answer in [checks-lost-answer.md](checks-lost-answer.md); one tab's panel changes, Undo and Redo before an answer are device-queue in [checks-device-queue.md](checks-device-queue.md).

## tabs

Two tabs of one browser have one deck open. They share its copy in IndexedDB: pending rows, the confirmed rows that mirror the server, the server revision those rows hold, and the head `changeHeld` reads in the same transaction as its write. `adoptRows` writes a deck the tab read and `confirmRows` writes an answer's rows, each in one transaction that first reads the copy's revision and writes nothing over a newer one; `confirmRows` numbers its rows after the copy's last confirmed row as it is then. Each tab moves its own mirror on for an answer only when it wrote every row of the batch itself (`takeEdits`), since it painted those; a row the other tab wrote leaves the mirror where it was, so the tab's follow reads the deck and paints it. A tab learns of the other tab's edits only through the server: its follow sees a newer revision, writes the deck into the copy and paints the copy. The editor starts that follow when the deck is shown. Follow still waits for the tab's own row send, and it does not wait for the opening index page, so an agent's edit paints while that page is on the wire. The first tab moves a text box; the second tab then makes its words bold in the design panel, planned against the copy. The first tab stays on the build it loaded. The second may reload once, into the same build or a newer one, which opens the copy at a higher IndexedDB version.

- `NoEditLost`: every edit a tab painted is pending in the copy or landed on the server, whichever tab sends it. Harm if broken: a tab writes over the other tab's pending row, or drops it unsent.
- `WordsKept`: the bold keeps the box's words. Harm if broken: the panel plans against a box the copy rebuilt with no words under it and writes the text back empty, the fault PR 549 fixed.
- `OlderStaysOut`: once a newer build opened the copy, the older tab writes nothing to it and shows the newer-version notice. Its rows stay for the newer tab, which sends them with the once they carry. Harm if broken: an older build writes rows the newer build's upgrade never saw.
- `SettledShowsServer`: once nothing is pending or on its way and each tab's mirror holds the server's revision, each tab paints the server's deck. Harm if broken: a tab that sent the other tab's pending row moves its mirror to that revision without painting it, so its follow never paints the edit (Move, SendTwo, ApplyTwo, AnswerTwo, FollowOne). Main broke it until the mirror moved on only for the tab's own rows.
- `NoOlderView`: the copy never loses a confirmed row. Harm if broken: an opening page keeps the server's answer (`openOnce`) while it fetches the deck's parts, and `openDeck` writes that answer over the confirmed rows another tab wrote for a newer revision (Move, SendOne, ReloadTwo, ReadTwo, ApplyOne, AnswerOne, AdoptTwo). The other tab's mirror still says it holds the newer revision, so it plans its next edit against the older copy and writes over the saved edit. In the app: tab A typed "Mine" and saw it saved, tab B opened, and tab A's Bold in the design panel saved "Before" over "Mine". Main broke it until the copy kept its revision.

Bounds: one box, one move in the first tab and one bold in the second after it, one reload of the second tab, one request on its way at a time from either tab, and no tab writes confirmed rows while a request is on its way. `crev` is the revision the copy holds; `wrote2` says the second tab's page wrote the bold row still pending, which a reload forgets. 484 states, 835 edges. Run: `pnpm check:specs tabs`.

Replay: `packages/live/src/tabs-replay.test.ts` imports `packages/live` a second time after `vi.resetModules`, so each tab has its own page, memory, mirror and lanes over the one fake IndexedDB of `browser-fixtures.ts`, with `memoryRemote` as the server behind a remote that holds each commit until the model's `Apply` and `Answer`. A reload imports a fresh copy; the newer build is a copy whose `format` is one higher, so it opens IndexedDB at the higher version, and once its bold landed the older tab's open answers `newer`, as `readRegister` answers for a row stamped past its format. Edits and paints go through `connect(memoryRemote())`, as the editor's `applyChange`, `commitBox` and `deviceCopy` do; sends, follows and the page's open go through each tab's own remote, as `useDeckOpen` does. It takes all 835 edges in 246 walks, and after every step compares the copy's pending and confirmed rows and their once, the revision the copy holds, the bold's words, the server's deck and revisions, whether the older tab was told of the newer build, and what each tab painted. Tests beside it pin the faults main had: the two counterexamples above, and a saved text the older answer dropped and the first tab's next edit then writes over. `packages/live/src/held.confirm.test.ts` covers `confirmRows` with the other tab writing the copy while an answer is on its way: a moved box's confirmed row an answer wrote over on main, an answer whose rows land after rows the other tab adopted meanwhile, and an older answer read after the other tab adopted a newer deck. Code: `connect` (`sendBatches`, `adopt`, `openDeck`, `follow`), `unlessNewer` and `heard` in `packages/live/src/cache.ts`, `changeHeld`, `editHeld`, `takeEdits`, `adoptRows`, `confirmRows`, `sealRows` and `heldView` in `held.ts`, `rowsFromRegister` and `played` in `deck-view.ts`, `fitConfirmed` and `logicalValues` in `store.ts`, `withSlides`, `landed` and `staleText` in `slide-replay.ts`, `markNewer`, `pageEdits`, `connectDatabase`, `openDatabase` and `transaction` in `device.ts`, `useDeckOpen`, `deviceCopy` and `applyChange` in `apps/web`.

Leaves out: a tab writing confirmed rows while a request is on its way. The model does not track row keys, and `confirmRows` numbers an answer's rows after the copy's last confirmed row inside its transaction; the three tests in `held.confirm.test.ts` cover the other tab adopting the same, an earlier and a newer revision before the answer is read. Also left out: two requests on their way at once, which reach the same server once as a resend does, and whose answers read in the other order leave the copy at the newer revision without the older batch's rows until the next follow; a lost answer and a reload with a request on its way, which lost-answer models; the localStorage journal of unsaved edits, which both tabs read; another device on a newer build, which the older tab meets only in the server's answer (`packages/live/src/format.test.ts`).
