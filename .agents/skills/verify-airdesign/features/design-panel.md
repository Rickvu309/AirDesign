# Set an element's design from the Design panel

With the Design panel open, selecting an element shows its sections from the trait tables instead of the slide's background, titled by what the element is (`Text box`, `Shape`, `Picture`, `Line`, `Group`, `Table`, `Chart`) and ordered for it by `panelOrder` in `apps/web/src/design/panel-order.ts`: a text box leads with Text, a shape with Fill, Line and Shape type, a picture with Picture and Crop, a connector with Line and Connector. Rare sections start folded to one line, a button in the section's heading with `aria-expanded`; a person's folding is kept for the tab (`sessionStorage`) for each kind apart. A plain rectangle picture shows no Shape type. Sections: Position and size in points with Rotation, the flips, Align and Distribute, the element's one Opacity, a shape's Shape (a preset and its adjusts), a shape's Fill (none, one color, a gradient, a pattern), the Line of a shape, picture or connector (its kind and color, width, dash, cap, compound, join and both arrowheads), the Effects of a shape, picture or connector (Shadow, Glow and Soft edge), the element's text with its colour, size, bold, italic, underline, alignment and bullets. Every row changes the deck through `planChange`, the same plan an agent's `set` meets, and each change is one Undo step. Clicking the empty slide shows the slide's background again.

## Sub-features

- `design-element` a text box selected: `Position and size` with X, Y, Width and Height, `Text` with the words, `Text color`, `Font size`, `Bold`, `Italic`, `Underline`, `Alignment` and `Bullets`.
- `design-element-changed` X, Bold, a theme colour and Center set from the panel. The canvas and the thumbnail paint each change at once, and the selection frame stays while focus is in the panel (`data-keeps-selection`).
- `design-table` a table selected: X and Y only, and `Cells`, one field per cell.
- `design-fill` a shape whose fill comes from its theme style, filled with a gradient: `Fill` with None, Color, Gradient and Pattern, the gradient's stops (position and color each), `Add stop`, its Direction (Linear with Angle, or From center) and `Rotate with shape`. `Reset fill` in the section's header goes back to the fill the layout, master or theme style gives, and shows only while the shape has its own fill. A fill is a row of trait fields (`{ v, patch }`) in the person's queue, painted from the exported bytes.
- `design-line` a shape whose line comes from its theme style, its Line width set to 6 pt: `Line` with None, Color, Gradient and Pattern, then Line width, Dash, Cap, Compound, Join, `Start arrow` with Start arrowhead, Start arrow width and Start arrow length, and `End arrow` with the same three named for the end. Every control in the panel has a name of its own, which `tests/browser/design-names.spec.ts` checks with `sharedNames` from `tests/browser/design-panel.ts`. Only the field set is written into the shape's own `a:ln`; the style still gives the rest. A shape with no line shows only the Line kind row.
- `design-effects` a shape whose shadow comes from its theme style, given a glow: `Effects` with `Shadow` (None, Outer, then Blur, Distance, Direction and Color), `Glow` (None, Glow, then Radius and Color) and `Soft edge` (None, Radius, then Radius). The first change copies the style's effect list into the shape byte for byte, then patches only the field set. A new shadow starts at 4 pt blur, 3 pt away at 45° in black at 40 % alpha, a new glow at 5 pt in accent1 at 40 % alpha, a new soft edge at 5 pt.
- `design-shape` a shape turned 30°, flipped and made a right arrow (on a text box `Shape type` starts folded): `Rotation` in degrees, `Flip horizontally` and `Flip vertically` as toggles, `Align` (Left, Center, Right over Top, Middle, Bottom, against the slide) and `Distribute` (Horizontally, Vertically, which centres one element on the slide), then `Shape type` with `Type`, every preset the painter draws but the bent and curved connector lines, and `Adjust`, one number per adjust of the picked preset at its default. A new preset starts from its own adjusts. Turns, flips and presets are rows painted from the exported bytes; Align and Distribute write X or Y.
- `design-opacity` a shape whose fill and line come from its theme style, its Opacity set to 60 %: one `Opacity` section just before Fill fades the fill, the line and the text together. It shows Mixed when they differ, and a group, a table or a chart has none. The export copies the style's fill as the shape's own before it writes the alpha, writes the line's shown colour with its alpha, and writes the alpha on every run's colour. A new colour keeps the opacity the field showed.
- `design-text-box` a text box set to Bottom with a 36 pt left margin: `Text box` with `Margins` (Left, Top, Right, Bottom in points), `Vertical alignment` (Top, Middle, Bottom), `Wrap text`, `Autofit` (Off, Shrink, Grow: Shrink narrows the lines and then lowers the size in PowerPoint's steps until the text fits, Grow makes the box taller around its anchor), `Text direction` (Horizontal, Rotate 90°, Rotate 270°) and `Columns`. `Text` gains `Font`, `Line spacing` in percent, and `Space before` and `Space after` in points.
- `design-picture` a photo in `picture-png.pptx` set to Grayscale: `Picture` with the icon search (no icons until a word is typed while the picture is a file; twelve to start once it is an icon), `Recolor` (None, Grayscale, Duotone with its Dark and Light colors), `Crop` (Left, Top, Right and Bottom in percent; the frame's edges move with the crop so the picture keeps its scale) and `Name and alt text` (Name, Alt text, Hide on slide) last. `Fill` gains an `Icon` case for a shape. The toolbar's `Add icon` (a star) opens the same search and places a one-inch icon.
- `color-picker-fill`, `color-picker-text`, `color-picker-background` the color picker open over a shape's fill, a text color and a slide's background: every color field, the text toolbar's `Text color`, the slide's background and the Theme section open one picker, titled with that property. `Template colors` are the theme colours the panel already lists, as plain swatches. `Others in this deck` starts with `Add color`, then the deck's RGB colours, and a chevron shows the rest. `Add color` opens a colour area, `Hue`, `Opacity`, `Hex` (six digits, with or without #) and, where the browser has one, `Pick a color from the screen`. PowerPoint's lighter and darker tints are in that picker, stored as the slot with lumMod and lumOff so a tint follows a theme change. The closed row names a theme colour and shows a hex for any other colour. The menu does not add a thirteenth theme colour.
- `design-theme` nothing selected, Accent 1 set to red on `clean.pptx`: `Theme` after the slide's `Background`, with the theme's twelve colours (Dark 1, Light 1, Dark 2, Light 2, Accent 1 to 6, Link, Visited link), each opening the color picker without theme colours, then `Heading font` and `Body font`. A change is a row on the deck's one theme node, `deck.theme`; the export writes it into every theme a slide uses (a deck with two masters has two), and every slide repaints from the exported theme, text on slides with their own edits included. Undo names it `Undo theme color` or `Undo theme font`.
- Undo names a panel change `Undo move`, `Undo resize`, `Undo text edit`, `Undo text format`, `Undo fill`, `Undo line`, `Undo effects`, `Undo text box`, `Undo rotate`, `Undo flip`, `Undo shape`, `Undo opacity`, `Undo picture`, `Undo crop`, `Undo rename`, `Undo alt text` or `Undo hide`, and an added icon `Undo add icon`.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- An open deck `/deck/<id>`: `Design` beside `Agent` in the toolbar, then a click on an element and Escape to leave its text.
- A change the element cannot take shows `Unable to make that change. The element stays as it was.`

## Gotchas

- Corners is a radius in pixels, with 9,525 EMU per pixel. It writes `geometry.shape`: `roundRect` with `adj = round(radius / shorter side × 100000)`, capped at 50000. Zero writes `rect`. Ellipses, arrows and custom geometry have no Corners row.

- A shape inside a group has no `Position and size`: the engine refuses box edits on a grouped child (`group-box`), pinned as `it.fails` in `panel-order.test.ts`.

- A font the browser lacks paints in the fallback face, so a theme font such as Georgia changes each run's font family but may look the same on a machine without it.

- A hidden element is not painted, so the canvas cannot select it again: Undo, or an agent's `set` with `identity: { hidden: false }`, shows it.

- The canvas does not soften a gradient fill's edge (`effect-art.test.ts` pins it as `it.fails`), so `Soft edge` on `nasa-cots-electronics.pptx`'s rectangles shows in the panel and the export but not on the canvas.

- The table states also show two canvas findings main has, an empty cell (`invisible-control`) and the table's frame inside the selection (`stacked-frames`).
- A build without the element panel reaches `design-element` and `design-table` with the slide's background panel, so the before side of a comparison has a picture.
- **Journey.** `$V journey <run> text-type`: an agent's `set` gives every title `text: { font: "Georgia" }` through `/mcp` and `read_deck` prints `text.font Georgia`, the editor paints the titles in Georgia, a person sets one title's line spacing, space before, Bottom and a 36 pt left margin, and the export and a new import keep them.
