# Set an element's design from the Design panel

With the Design panel open, selecting an element shows its sections from the trait tables instead of the slide's background, titled by what the element is (`Text box`, `Shape`, `Picture`, `Line`, `Group`, `Table`, `Chart`) and ordered for it by `panelOrder` in `apps/web/src/design/panel-order.ts`: a text box leads with Text, a shape with Fill, Line and Shape type, a picture with Picture and Crop, a connector with Line and Connector. Rare sections start folded to one line, a button in the section's heading with `aria-expanded`; a person's folding is kept for the tab (`sessionStorage`) for each kind apart. A plain rectangle picture shows no Shape type. Sections: Position and size in points with Rotation, the flips, Align and Distribute, the element's one Opacity, a shape's Shape (a preset and its adjusts), a shape's Fill (none, one color, a gradient, a pattern), the Line of a shape, picture or connector (its kind and color, width, dash, cap, compound, join and both arrowheads), the Effects of a shape, picture or connector (Shadow, Glow and Soft edge), the element's text with its colour, size, bold, italic, underline, alignment and bullets. Every row changes the deck through `planChange`, the same plan an agent's `set` meets, and each change is one Undo step. Clicking the empty slide shows the slide's background again.

## Sub-features

- `design-many-text` shows Multi-edit text for two selected text boxes. The field shows Mixed for different words. Blur or Ctrl/Cmd+Enter replaces the words in one Undo step. Escape discards the draft.
- `design-many-line` shows the shared panel for a rectangle and a line. It omits Opacity, Corners and the None border preset.
- `design-element` a text box selected: `Position and size` with X, Y, Width and Height, `Text` with the words, `Text color`, `Font size`, `Bold`, `Italic`, `Underline`, `Alignment` and `Bulleted list`.
- `design-element-changed` X, Bold, a theme colour and Center set from the panel. The canvas and the thumbnail paint each change at once, and the selection frame stays while focus is in the panel (`data-keeps-selection`).
- `design-table` a table selected: X and Y only, and `Cells`, one field per cell.
- `design-fill` a shape whose fill comes from its theme style, filled with a gradient: `Fill` with None, Color, Gradient and Pattern, the gradient's stops (position and color each), `Add stop`, its Direction (Linear with Angle, or From center) and `Rotate with shape`. `Reset fill` in the section's header goes back to the fill the layout, master or theme style gives, and shows only while the shape has its own fill. A fill is a row of trait fields (`{ v, patch }`) in the person's queue, painted from the exported bytes.
- `design-border` a shape whose border comes from its theme style, its Border width set to 6 pt, in design mode: one `Border` section with None, Solid, Dashed and Dotted, then `Color` with None, Solid, Gradient and Pattern, Border width, Dash, Cap, Compound, Join, `Start arrow` with Start arrowhead, Start arrow width and Start arrow length, and `End arrow` with the same three named for the end. Slides mode shows the presets, then Color and Border width. Every control in the panel has a name of its own, which `tests/browser/design-names.spec.ts` checks with `sharedNames` from `tests/browser/design-panel.ts`. Only the field set is written into the shape's own `a:ln`; the style still gives the rest. A shape with no border shows only the presets.
- `design-effects` a shape whose shadow comes from its theme style, given a glow: `Effects` lists the kinds the layer has, in DrawingML order, each with its settings and Remove. Kinds it does not have stay off the list. `Add` offers the missing kinds (shadow, inner shadow, glow, soft edge, blur), one of each. The first change copies the style's effect list into the shape byte for byte, then patches only the field set. A new shadow or inner shadow starts at 4 pt blur, 3 pt away at 45° in black at 40 % alpha, a new glow at 5 pt in accent1 at 40 % alpha, a new soft edge or blur at 5 pt. A kept effect graph shows as one read-only row, Effect graph, and Add stays empty.
- `design-shape` a shape turned 30°, flipped and made a right arrow (on a text box `Shape type` starts folded): `Rotation` in degrees, `Flip horizontally` and `Flip vertically` as toggles, `Align` (Left, Center, Right over Top, Middle, Bottom, against the slide) and `Distribute` (Horizontally, Vertically, which centres one element on the slide), then `Shape type` with `Type`, every preset the painter draws but the bent and curved connector lines, and `Adjust`, one number per adjust of the picked preset at its default. A new preset starts from its own adjusts. Turns, flips and presets are rows painted from the exported bytes; Align and Distribute write X or Y.
- `design-opacity` a shape whose fill and line come from its theme style, its Opacity set to 60 %: one `Opacity` section just before Fill fades the fill, the line and the text together. It shows Mixed when they differ, and a group, a table or a chart has none. The export copies the style's fill as the shape's own before it writes the alpha, writes the line's shown colour with its alpha, and writes the alpha on every run's colour. A new colour keeps the opacity the field showed.
- `design-text-box` a text box set to Bottom with a 36 pt left margin: `Text box` with `Margins` (Left, Top, Right, Bottom in points), `Vertical alignment` (Top, Middle, Bottom), `Wrap text`, `Autofit` (Off, Shrink, Grow: Shrink narrows the lines and then lowers the size in PowerPoint's steps until the text fits, Grow makes the box taller around its anchor), `Text direction` (Horizontal, Rotate 90°, Rotate 270°) and `Columns`. `Text` gains `Font`, `Line height` in percent, and `Space before` and `Space after` in points.
- `design-picture` a photo in `picture-png.pptx` set to Grayscale: `Picture` with the icon search (no icons until a word is typed while the picture is a file; twelve to start once it is an icon), `Recolor` (None, Grayscale, Duotone with its Dark and Light colors), `Crop` (Left, Top, Right and Bottom in percent; the frame's edges move with the crop so the picture keeps its scale) and `Name and alt text` (Name, Alt text, Hide on slide) last. `Fill` gains an `Icon` case for a shape. The toolbar's `Add icon` (a star) opens the same search and places an icon 1.5 times the slide's body text size, or one inch on a slide with no text. `panel-icon` is that icon selected: its `Fill` shows `Color` (the template's text color to start) and `Weight` (Lucide's 2, steps 1 to 3) in one row under the fill mode; a photo shows neither.
- `color-picker-fill`, `color-picker-text`, `color-picker-background` the color picker open over a shape's fill, a text color and a slide's background: every color field, the text toolbar's `Text color`, the slide's background and the Theme section open one picker, titled with that property. `Template colors` are the theme colours the panel already lists, as plain swatches. `Others in this deck` starts with `Add color`, then the deck's RGB colours, and a chevron shows the rest. `Add color` opens a colour area, `Hue`, `Opacity`, `Hex` (six digits, with or without #) and, where the browser has one, `Pick a color from the screen`. PowerPoint's lighter and darker tints are in that picker, stored as the slot with lumMod and lumOff so a tint follows a theme change. The closed row names a theme colour and shows a hex for any other colour. The menu does not add a thirteenth theme colour.
- `design-theme` nothing selected, Accent 1 set to red on `clean.pptx`: `Theme` after the slide's `Background`, with the theme's twelve colours (Dark 1, Light 1, Dark 2, Light 2, Accent 1 to 6, Link, Visited link), each opening the color picker without theme colours, then `Heading font` and `Body font`. A change is a row on that template style's node, `deck.templateStyleN`; the export writes it into the PowerPoint theme part that style owns, and every slide that uses it repaints, text on slides with their own edits included. Undo names it `Undo template color` or `Undo template font`.
- Undo names a panel change `Undo move`, `Undo resize`, `Undo text edit`, `Undo text format`, `Undo fill`, `Undo border`, `Undo effects`, `Undo text box`, `Undo rotate`, `Undo flip`, `Undo shape`, `Undo opacity`, `Undo picture`, `Undo crop`, `Undo rename`, `Undo alt text` or `Undo hide`, and an added icon `Undo add icon`.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- An open deck `/deck/<id>`: `Design` beside `Agent` in the toolbar, then a click on an element and Escape to leave its text.
- A change the element cannot take shows `Unable to make that change. The element stays as it was.`

## Gotchas

- Corners is a radius in pixels, with 9,525 EMU per pixel. It writes `geometry.shape`: `roundRect` with `adj = round(radius / shorter side × 100000)`, capped at 50000. Zero writes `rect`. Ellipses, arrows and custom geometry have no Corners row. The field has a list of 0, 4, 8, 16, 24 and 32. A typed number that is not in the list stays in the field with nothing marked.

- Opacity on a text box, shape or picture has a checkerboard in the field and a list of 100, 75, 50, 25, 10 and 0. A line's folded Opacity is the field only, and a slide has no Opacity row. Border shows Color and Border width once the style is solid, dashed or dotted. The width list is 1, 2, 4, 6 and 8, the field shows the number with a line glyph and no unit, and Color takes the room beside it. A line's Border has Solid, Dashed and Dotted; a shape or picture also has None, which hides Color and Border width. Shadow's three strengths have no names on the button face. While one is pressed, a caption reads Opacity, the slider is named Opacity slider, and the percent field is named Shadow opacity. Both write the shadow colour's alpha, and the strength stays pressed. None, or a shadow Effects has changed, hides that row. The lists and the three strength sizes are the panel's own stand-ins.

- A shape inside a group has no `Position and size`: the engine refuses box edits on a grouped child (`group-box`), pinned as `it.fails` in `panel-order.test.ts`.

- A font the browser lacks paints in the fallback face, so a theme font such as Georgia changes each run's font family but may look the same on a machine without it.

- A hidden element is not painted, so the canvas cannot select it again: Undo, or an agent's `set` with `identity: { hidden: false }`, shows it.

- The canvas does not soften a gradient fill's edge (`effect-art.test.ts` pins it as `it.fails`), so `Soft edge` on `nasa-cots-electronics.pptx`'s rectangles shows in the panel and the export but not on the canvas.

- The table states also show two canvas findings main has, an empty cell (`invisible-control`) and the table's frame inside the selection (`stacked-frames`).
- A build without the element panel reaches `design-element` and `design-table` with the slide's background panel, so the before side of a comparison has a picture.
- **Journey.** `$V journey <run> text-type`: an agent's `set` gives every title `text: { font: "Georgia" }` through `/mcp` and `read_deck` prints `text.font Georgia`, the editor paints the titles in Georgia, a person sets one title's line height, space before, Bottom and a 36 pt left margin, and the export and a new import keep them.
