# Checks on undoing a step on a layer's place

The entry has the same parts as those in [checks-doom.md](checks-doom.md). [checks-undo-steps.md](checks-undo-steps.md) holds the undo and redo stacks it builds on.

## undo-strays

One device's undo and redo stacks over steps on the card K's place, beside a move by another writer. The slide holds frame F, and K is in F or on the slide. K's x, from -2 to 3 because an undo may carry it from one parent to the other, is measured in its parent, so the same numbers are two places in two parents. Before the first press the person makes two steps: a nudge, which writes x, or a drag into the other parent, which writes the parent and x. Once the person has a step, another writer may act once: move K to the other parent and write no x, as an agent's `move` does, reorder K inside its parent, which writes the parent and keeps it, as Ctrl+] does, or write x. An undo writes x when its step or a press that landed wrote it last (`invert`), and the parent for a drag that wrote it last. When it writes x but not the parent, and K no longer sits in the frame it sat in before the step, the frame the x is measured in, `onStandingLayers` treats x as for a move it leaves out: K keeps its x, y and turn, and the rest of the box comes back. When it writes the parent but not x, because the other writer wrote x after the step, it writes the x that keeps K where it is seen in the parent it goes back to (`replanted` in `packages/doom/src/replant.ts`). With nothing to write the press takes the step off and answers `nothing`. Up to three presses.

- `PutsBackWhereSeen`: a press that lands moves K away from where the person saw it only by writing an x the step or a press owns, and only when it writes K's parent too or K still sits in the frame it sat in before the step, the frame that x is measured in. The model judges it against that frame, not against the frame the rule compares, so a wrong rule fails it. A rule that asks about the frame the step left it in fails it once the other writer reorders K there: ungroup F, the other tab presses Ctrl+], Ctrl+Z gives K F's numbers (verdict 1542-c5ba7cd, finding 1). An undo that writes the parent and not x fails it too: Aira's run moves K out of F, the person nudges K, Undo on the run puts K back in F with the slide's numbers and it jumps by F's offset (verdict 1542-f4d7b42, follow-up 1). Harm if broken: the person resizes a card in a frame, an agent run moves it onto the slide, and Ctrl+Z writes the frame's x and y onto the slide, so the card jumps across it (verdict 1532-cfcc610, finding 1).
- `NeverCoversTheirs`: a press writes over the x the other writer wrote last only to keep K seen where it is.
- `NeverStuck`: every press takes a step off its stack.
- The replay reads, at every edge, K's parent and x, the edit that wrote each last and what the press answered from the real device deck, and that the server holds what the tab shows.

Bounds: x is from -2 to 3, a nudge, a drag or the other writer's write sets it to 0 or 1, two steps before the first press, one action by the other writer at any time after the person's first step, three presses. 343 states. Run: `pnpm check:specs undo-strays`. Replay: `tests/undo-strays-replay.test.ts` drives all 342 edges through the real `createDoomDeck`, `openDoomDeck` and `memoryDoomRemote`, the person's tab and another tab on one server. `packages/live/src/doom-undo-stray-box.test.ts` holds the cases beside it: the size and flip that come back, a run's undo, the chain through a deleted frame, a reorder inside the frame the step moved K to, two people who group, nudge and undo in turn, and Undo on a run that moved K out of its frame after the person nudged it, through a frame the person turned. `packages/doom/src/invert-replant.test.ts` holds the box written for a turned frame and a line's point ends. `tests/browser/editor-undo-deleted-layer.spec.ts` holds the group and the ungroup as keys in two tabs, and `tests/browser/editor-undo-run-box.spec.ts` the Ctrl+Z of the tab that grouped after the other tab nudged; `packages/live/src/doom-undo-stray-box.test.ts` holds the run undo after a nudge.

Leaves out: deleted frames (undo-deleted-frames), the y axis and the turn of a frame, which the model does not have and the unit and live tests do, a line's point ends, which follow the same rule in `lineLeftInPlace`, and a step that moves several layers.
