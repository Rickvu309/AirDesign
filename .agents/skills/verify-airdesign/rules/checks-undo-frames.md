# Checks on undoing a step that frames a layer

The entry has the same parts as those in [checks-doom.md](checks-doom.md). [checks-undo-steps.md](checks-undo-steps.md) holds the undo and redo stacks it builds on.

## undo-frames

One device's undo and redo stacks over a step that frames a layer. The slide holds frames F and H, F holds G, and G holds the card K. Before the first press the person makes two steps: a drag of K, while it shows, into G or H when that frame shows, or once, framing K (Ctrl+Alt+G, `wrapWrites` in `apps/web/src/doom-editor/group-writes.ts`): one edit makes the frame X where K is and moves K into it. Once the person has a step, another tab may act once, at any time: delete F, G or H, drag K into G, H or X, or drag H into X, each while the layer and the frame show and the frame is not inside the layer. An undo writes each part of the top step whose parent the step or a turn that landed wrote last (`invert`). It leaves out a move whose frame, or a frame above it, is deleted, with the other writes to that layer, and the delete of a frame that would still hold a layer: one whose move it left out, or one someone moved in after the step (`onStandingLayers` and `heldFrames` in `packages/doom/src/standing-layers.ts`). With nothing left it takes the step off and answers `nothing`; a turn that lands pushes the step that writes it back on the other stack. Up to three presses.

This copies Figma, where deleting a frame deletes the layers in it: an undo of framing a card inside a frame someone else deleted does nothing, and their undo brings the frame back with the new frame and the card in it.

- `NeverLosesALayer`: a press never deletes a frame that a layer is left inside. Harm if broken: the person frames the card, another tab deletes F, and Ctrl+Z deletes X with the card in it. The other tab's Ctrl+Z brings F and G back, and the card is gone in both tabs (verdict 1514-70f3dd1, finding 1). Or another tab drags H into the person's new frame, and Ctrl+Z deletes H with it.
- `NeverLosesAStep`: a press that answers nothing takes off only a step whose every part is spent: someone else wrote it since, its frame is gone, or it would delete a frame that still holds a layer. A turn that puts K back puts it where the step found it, and K shows there.
- `NeverCoversTheirs`: a turn never writes a parent the other tab wrote last.
- `NeverStuck`: every press takes a step off its stack.
- The replay reads, at every edge, the three parents, the edit that wrote each last, which frame is deleted and what the press answered from the real device deck, and that the server holds what the tab shows.

Bounds: two steps before the first press, at most one of them framing K, one action by the other tab at any time after the person's first step, three presses. 474 states. Run: `pnpm check:specs undo-frames`. Replay: `tests/undo-frames-replay.test.ts` drives all 474 edges through the real `createDoomDeck`, `openDoomDeck` and `memoryDoomRemote`, the person's tab and another tab on one server: each edge replays the shortest path to its source on a fresh deck, then the edge. `packages/doom/src/invert.test.ts`, `packages/doom/src/invert-run.test.ts`, `packages/live/src/doom-undo-deleted-layer.test.ts` and `tests/browser/doom-undo-deleted-layer.spec.ts` hold the cases beside it: the frame's own frame deleted, a frame above it deleted, another tab's layer in the new frame, the card's box, which stays the one its new frame gave it, the same rule for an agent run that frames a layer, and two real tabs.

Leaves out: moves between nested frames and the cycles they make (undo-moves); the boxes the step writes (`invert.test.ts`, `doom-undo-deleted-layer.test.ts` and the browser case); a frame made with more than one layer in it, and a frame inside the new frame, which only widen the same paths (`invert.test.ts` holds a frame inside one); property writes (undo-steps); an undo queued offline (verdict 1507-1fb8aa5, finding 2, still open); agent runs (doom-runs; `invert-run.test.ts` holds the same rule for a run's undo).
