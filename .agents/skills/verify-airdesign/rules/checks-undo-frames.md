# Checks on undoing a step that frames a layer

The entry has the same parts as those in [checks-doom.md](checks-doom.md). [checks-undo-steps.md](checks-undo-steps.md) holds the undo and redo stacks it builds on.

## undo-frames

One device's undo and redo stacks over a step that frames a layer. The slide holds frames F and H, F holds G, and G holds the card K. Before the first press the person makes two steps: a drag of K, while it shows, into G or H when that frame shows, or once, framing K (Ctrl+Alt+G, `wrapped` in `apps/web/src/editor/arrange-intents.ts`): one edit makes the frame X where K is and moves K into it. The editor sends the frame intent through `intentWrites` in `apps/web/src/editor/editor-intents.ts` to `doom.intentWrites`. The replay starts with the compiled writes. Once the person has a step, another tab may act once, at any time: delete F, G or H, drag K into G, H or X, or drag H into X, each while the layer and the frame show and the frame is not inside the layer, or, before the first press, delete X while it shows. An undo writes each part of the top step whose parent the step or a turn that landed wrote last (`invert`). It leaves out a move whose frame is deleted, or, while the layer shows, a frame above it, and the delete of a frame that would still hold a layer: one whose move it left out, or one someone moved in after the step (`onStandingLayers` and `heldFrames` in `packages/doom/src/standing-layers.ts`). A layer that does not show goes back under the deleted frame and comes back with it. With nothing left it takes the step off and answers `nothing`; a turn that lands pushes the step that writes it back on the other stack. Up to three presses.

Where the card's own frame is deleted this copies Figma, where deleting a frame deletes the layers in it: an undo of framing a card inside a frame someone else deleted does nothing, and their undo brings the frame back with the new frame and the card in it. Where a frame above it is deleted, the card does not show, so Ctrl+Z puts it back into its frame and deletes the new one, and their undo brings the card back as it was before the framing.

- `NeverLosesALayer`: a press never deletes a frame that a layer is left inside. Harm if broken: the person frames the card, another tab deletes F, and Ctrl+Z deletes X with the card in it. The other tab's Ctrl+Z brings F and G back, and the card is gone in both tabs (verdict 1514-70f3dd1, finding 1). Or another tab drags H into the person's new frame, and Ctrl+Z deletes H with it.
- `NeverLosesAStep`: a press that answers nothing takes off only a step whose every part is spent: someone else wrote it since, its frame is gone, or it would delete a frame that still holds a layer. A turn that puts K back puts it where the step found it, where it shows if it showed before the press.
- `NeverCoversTheirs`: a turn never writes a parent the other tab wrote last.
- `NeverStuck`: every press takes a step off its stack.
- The replay reads, at every edge, the three parents, the edit that wrote each last, which frame is deleted and what the press answered from the real device deck, and that the server holds what the tab shows.

Bounds: two steps before the first press, at most one of them framing K, one action by the other tab at any time after the person's first step, its delete of X only before the first press, three presses. 497 states. Run: `pnpm check:specs undo-frames`. Replay: `tests/undo-frames-replay.test.ts` drives all 497 edges through the real `createDoomDeck`, `openDoomDeck` and `memoryDoomRemote`, the person's tab and another tab on one server: each edge replays the shortest path to its source on a fresh deck, then the edge. `packages/doom/src/invert.test.ts`, `packages/doom/src/invert-run.test.ts`, `packages/live/src/doom-undo-deleted-layer.test.ts` and `tests/browser/editor-undo-deleted-layer.spec.ts` hold the cases beside it: the frame's own frame deleted, a frame above it deleted, another tab's layer in the new frame, the card's box, which stays the one its new frame gave it while the card stays in it, the same rule for an agent run that frames a layer, and two real tabs.

Leaves out: moves between nested frames and the cycles they make (undo-moves); a deleted card and the other tab's undo of its delete (undo-deleted-frames); the other tab's delete of X after the first press, which only widens the same paths and goes over 500 states; the boxes the step writes (`invert.test.ts`, `doom-undo-deleted-layer.test.ts` and the browser case); a frame made with more than one layer in it, and a frame inside the new frame, which only widen the same paths (`invert.test.ts` holds a frame inside one); property writes (undo-steps); an undo queued offline (undo-offline, over a deleted title only); agent runs (doom-runs; `invert-run.test.ts` holds the same rule for a run's undo).

## undo-deleted-frames

One device's undo and redo stacks over steps on a card K whose frame, or the frame above it, another tab deletes and may bring back. The slide holds frames F and H, F holds G, and G holds K. K's colour stands for every write that does not place K in its frame: a fill, its words, its size. Before the first press the person makes two steps, each while K shows: a drag of K into H, which may also paint K in the same edit, as one edit of an agent run can, or a delete of K. Once the person has a step, another tab may act once: delete F or G, or paint K while it shows. After its delete it may press Ctrl+Z once, which brings the frame back. An undo writes each part of the top step whose last writer is the step or a turn that landed (`invert`). It leaves out a move of K into a deleted frame, or, while K shows, into a frame under a deleted one (`onStandingLayers` in `packages/doom/src/standing-layers.ts`); a K that does not show goes back under the deleted frame, and a deleted K even into the deleted frame itself (`write` in `packages/doom/src/apply-edit.ts`). It puts K's colour back while K stands or comes back in the same undo, whether or not its move lands (`boxLeftInPlace`). With nothing left it takes the step off and answers `nothing`; a turn that lands pushes the step that writes it back on the other stack. Up to three presses.

Strict Figma would lose the card when the person deletes it from G and another tab deletes F: their delete takes G with F, and an undo into a deleted frame does nothing. AirDesign puts the card back into G, so their undo brings F, G and the card back, as `main` did. A card deleted from G while the other tab deletes G itself goes back into G too: the deck lets a write that takes back a delete move a deleted layer into a deleted frame, where it stays hidden until the frame comes back, when its base matches K’s delete rev (verdict 1514-d30cef9, finding 6). A move of deleted K into deleted G is refused `missing` with an absent base. With an older base, Doom drops K’s writes and lands the edit. See [undo-pending-moves](#undo-pending-moves) for both cases.

- `NeverLosesALayer`: when the other tab brings back the frame it deleted, a K the person deleted comes back with it, or a step on the person's stacks still brings it back, the frame K was deleted from among them. Harm if broken: delete the card in Outer > Inner, another tab deletes Outer, Ctrl+Z answers nothing, and their Ctrl+Z brings Outer and Inner back without the card, in both tabs (verdict 1514-a78b019, finding 2); or delete the card in Inner, another tab deletes Inner, and their Ctrl+Z brings Inner back without it (verdict 1514-d30cef9, finding 6).
- `NeverDropsAVisibleEdit`: a press on a step that changed K's colour puts it back, whatever happens to K's move, unless someone else changed it since or K stays deleted. Harm if broken: an agent run moves the card out of P into Q and paints it red, another tab deletes P, and Undo on the run leaves the card red and says nothing was left to undo (verdict 1514-a78b019, finding 1).
- `NeverLosesAStep`: a press that answers nothing takes off only a step whose every part is spent, and a turn that puts K back puts it where the step found it, where it shows if it showed before the press.
- `NeverCoversTheirs`: a turn never writes K's parent or colour when the other tab wrote it last.
- `NeverStuck`: every press takes a step off its stack.
- The replay reads, at every edge, K's parent and colour, the edit that wrote each last, which frame is deleted and what the press answered from the real device deck, and that the server holds what the tab shows.

Bounds: two steps before the first press, drags into H only, one action by the other tab after the person's first step and its one undo of a delete, three presses. 400 states. Run: `pnpm check:specs undo-deleted-frames`. Replay: `tests/undo-deleted-frames-replay.test.ts` drives all 473 edges through the real `createDoomDeck`, `openDoomDeck` and `memoryDoomRemote`, the person's tab and another tab on one server, with the other tab's Ctrl+Z through its own `undo`. `packages/doom/src/invert.test.ts`, `packages/doom/src/invert-run.test.ts`, `packages/live/src/doom-undo-deleted-layer.test.ts` and `tests/browser/editor-undo-deleted-layer.spec.ts` hold the cases beside it: words and size beside the colour, a line's ends, the box left out when only its place changed, an agent run's undo and the layers it names as `left`, a text box emptied and deleted, and two real tabs.

Leaves out: a drag of K into G and the other tab's delete of H, which only widen the same paths and go over 500 states; the other tab's delete of K and a move of K the person's tab sends before it sees that delete (undo-pending-moves); frames (undo-frames) and moves between nested frames (undo-moves); agent runs (doom-runs; `invert-run.test.ts` and `doom-undo-deleted-layer.test.ts` hold the same rule for a run's undo).

## undo-pending-moves

The slide holds frame P, and P holds the card K. The person may queue one reorder of K within P while offline, before seeing the other tab's changes. The reorder carries no base. A second path starts with a saved move within P and queues its inverse offline. The other tab deletes K, and may delete P too, before the inverse replays. The inverse still carries the move's parent rev. Online, the person may delete K once while it shows and press Ctrl+Z once on that step. The other tab can delete K or P twice in total and undo its own deletes.

The earlier promise let an undo restore deleted K, even into deleted P, when the write named the edit it replaced. The current promise requires a base matching K's exact parent rev at deletion. An older base drops K's parent and accompanying writes, and the edit lands whether P stands or is deleted. An absent base drops K's writes if P stands and is refused `missing` if P is deleted. This is the intended change in d9a085f89, recorded in `packages/doom/src/parent-landing.rule.md`. `queueEditOn` queues the pending writes, `onTop` preserves the older or absent base on deleted K, and `staysDeleted` and `takesBackDelete` use `parentLanding` when `landEdit` applies the batch.

This copies Figma, where each Ctrl+Z takes back one step: the other tab deletes K and then P, and its two undos bring back P and then K.

- `OneStepAtATime`: an undo that brings P back shows K only when the delete of P hid it, or an undo has put K back into P since. Harm if broken: the person reorders K offline, the other tab deletes K and then P, and the reorder lands when the person comes back online. Their Ctrl+Z brings back P and K together, and their next Ctrl+Z does nothing (verdict 1533-b022c58, finding 1).
- `NeverLosesALayer`: when P comes back, a K the person deleted, which nobody wrote since, still has the person's step that brings it back. Harm if broken: the person deletes K, the other tab deletes P, the person's Ctrl+Z does nothing, and their Ctrl+Z brings P back without K (verdict 1514-d30cef9, finding 6).
- The replay reads, at every edge, K's and P's parents, the edit that wrote each last, and whether the queued edit is pending, landed or set aside with `missing`, from the real device decks, and that the person's tab holds what the server holds once it is online.

Bounds: one pending reorder or one queued inverse of a saved move, one delete and one press by the person, two deletes by the other tab and its presses on them. The inverse branch delivers the queued edit after K's delete and before the other tab undoes either delete. 399 states and 445 replay edges, up from 391 and 436. Run: `pnpm check:specs undo-pending-moves`. Replay: `tests/undo-pending-moves-replay.test.ts` drives all 445 edges through the real `createDoomDeck`, `openDoomDeck` and `memoryDoomRemote`. It checks that the queued inverse predates K's delete, its edit row lands, and the delete writer and the moved box survive in both the tab's projection and the server fold.

Leaves out: an inverse delivered before the remote delete or after the other tab undoes it, redo (undo-deleted-frames), frames above P (undo-deleted-frames), and agent runs. `packages/doom/src/parent-landing.test.ts` also covers a parent made in the same batch, missing parents and a queued inverse.

Planted fault on the current replay: accepting any base up to the delete rev in `parentLanding` made six edges disagree. The projected parent of K was P where the replay expected it to stay deleted.
