Usage, explained.

Agent tools cost {{agentRate}} unit per estimated unit. Aira costs {{airaRate}}. The estimate is shown before admission; this is not a promise of a fixed number of decks.

Credits are integer USD cents with no expiry. Reset options and their amounts are shown in Settings. Bonus credits are used before paid credits. Credit purchases require at least {{minimumPurchase}}. Up to three enabled automatic resets per natural week.

Paid resets are not available for public use. Provider sandbox verification is pending. No payment is collected on this site.

Time zone changes take effect at your current reset instant and do not grant extra usage. Platform-caused failures are refunded once; user errors are not.

PPTX export is locally checked. Imported content may remain preserved or flagged rather than editable. Windows and Mac PowerPoint acceptance is pending.
